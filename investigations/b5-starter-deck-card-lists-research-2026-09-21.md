# Stored advisory intake — B5 CCG starter deck card lists (research findings)

**Purpose and Origin.** This document was produced by Perplexity AI (version
unknown) on 2026-09-21, in response to a request to locate the Babylon 5 CCG
Premier Edition fixed starter-deck card lists for the four original races
(Human/Earth Alliance, Centauri, Minbari, Narn). It is stored here as
**advisory material only** — not canonical, not a ruling, and no task, DEC, or
promotion is inferred from storage. Assessed and stored by big-pickle
(`opencode/big-pickle`) under the analyze-assess-record-store workflow; the
original authorship is preserved above.

**Storage assessment (advisory, non-canonical).** The report was compared
against the live card dataset in `b5ccg/resources/cards/` (`premiere.json`,
446 cards; `deluxe.json`, 383 cards):

- **Card-level data corroborated.** The report's "All Fixed Cards" table has
  146 rows and matches, row-for-row, the **146 `FIXED`-rarity cards in
  `premiere.json`**. 145 of 146 titles match exactly with **0 type
  mismatches**; the single title variant is the repo's
  `"Level the Playing Field 3+9"` vs the report's `"Level the Playing Field"`.
  So the report's per-card names and type classifications agree with the repo
  dataset.
- **Structural claims NOT supported.** The report's prose asserts **50 fixed
  cards per deck / 200 fixed cards total**. Its own main table has 146 rows,
  and its appendices list **81 (Human) / 79 (Centauri) / 81 (Minbari) /
  82 (Narn)** cards — sums of 323, not 200. The repo data carries 146 `FIXED`
  cards in Premier Edition, not 200. The 50/200 figures are unsupported and
  internally contradicted.
- **Per-deck membership is a derivation, not sourced data.** The report's
  per-deck checkmarks reproduce exactly "that race's `FIXED` cards + all
  `ANY`/`NEUTRAL` `FIXED` cards" from the repo: Human 22+57+2=81,
  Centauri 20+57+2=79, Minbari 22+57+2=81, Narn 23+57+2=82. This is
  mechanically derivable from faction counts and is not evidence of the
  original printed starter-deck contents.
- **Minor.** Report says "445 total cards"; repo Premier Edition has 446.
- **Citation hygiene is poor.** No per-card sourcing; the references include
  several unrelated Magic: The Gathering "Fifth Edition" pages.

Verdict: **advisory research, partially corroborated at card level, with an
unsupported central structural claim.** Not to be treated as canonical card
data or as authority on original starter-deck composition.

### Starter Deck Composition

According to the official rules and product information:

- **Each starter deck**: 60 cards total
    - **50 fixed cards** (race-specific commons marked "Fixed" rarity)
    - **10 random uncommon/rare cards** (inserted to make each deck unique)
- **Four starter races**: Earth Alliance (Human), Centauri Republic, Minbari Federation, Narn Regime[^1_5][^1_6]
- **Ambassador card**: Each race-specific starter included that race's Ambassador character card as one of the 50 fixed cards (Sinclair for Earth, Londo for Centauri, Delenn for Minbari, G'Kar for Narn)[^1_7]


### Notes for Recreation

1. **Fixed cards** were exclusive to starter decks in the Premier Edition and marked with "F" rarity
2. The **Deluxe Edition** (1998) redistributed many fixed cards into regular packs, but some were removed or changed
3. Card text and effects were updated between Premier and Deluxe editions, though Premier cards remained tournament-legal[^1_2][^1_3]

## 2. Background and Context

### 2.1 Project Origin

The Babylon 5 Collectible Card Game was published by Precedence Entertainment in 1997 as a collectible card game based on the Babylon 5 science fiction television franchise. The game was notable for supporting multiplayer gameplay (2–10 players) and featured four primary playable races from the series.

### 2.2 Product Structure

The Premier Edition consisted of:

- **445 total cards** in the base set
- **60-card starter decks** (race-specific)
- **8-card booster packs**
- **Four race-specific starter decks**: Earth Alliance, Centauri Republic, Minbari Federation, and Narn Regime[^2_1][^2_2]

Each starter deck contained:

- **50 fixed common cards** (race-tailored, marked "Fixed" rarity)
- **10 random uncommon/rare cards** (inserted for deck uniqueness)[^2_3][^2_1]
