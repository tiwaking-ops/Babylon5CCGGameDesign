## What landed

A damage-state marker in `HandPanel.drawCard`, mirroring the B5-0516
board marker via the same B5-0368 Card base-class API
(`getDamageTokens`, `getSevereDamageTokens`, `isNeutralized`):

* **Same compact convention** as the board: `DMG:<n>` (severe appended
  `+<s>`), `NEUT`, or `NEUT <n>+<s>` — red (255,80,80) bold 7pt.
* **Right-aligned on the stats baseline** (`y + 50`) so it cannot
  collide with the left-aligned D/I/P/L, Military, or conflict-reward
  stat string on that line — no overlap possible by construction.
* **Drawn after the dim overlay** so the state stays readable on dimmed
  cards (the same never-hidden principle as the B5-0361 ELIGIBLE tag);
  skipped for face-down cards (identity + state opaque, B5-0381).
* **Dormant in normal play** — damage applies to in-play cards and hand
  cards are fresh — but the readout fires if the engine ever applies
  damage to a hand-held card. This closes the B5-0504 gap-table entry
  that named HandPanel alongside GameBoardPanel.
