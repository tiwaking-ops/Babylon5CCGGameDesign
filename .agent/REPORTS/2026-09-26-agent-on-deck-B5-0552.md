## Collision analysis — GameBoardPanel mini-cards

### Collision 1: Damage badge vs Contingency badge (CRITICAL, 46px width)

**Severity: HIGH — data loss on 46px mini-cards**

Both badges sit at `y + h - 2` (same visual line, 1px from bottom border):
- Contingency badge: right-aligned at `x + w - g.getFontMetrics().stringWidth("C#") - 2`
- Damage badge: left-aligned at `x + 2`

On a 46px-wide mini-card:
- Worst-case damage string: `DMG:3+2` = 33px (8pt bold 7, ~5.5px/char × 6 chars)
- Contingency string: `C3` = ~14px (8pt bold)
- Total needed: 33 + 14 + 4px (min gap) = 51px
- Available: 46px
- **Overflow: 5px → overlap**

On a 60px wide card (large/ambassador), the gap is sufficient (60 - 51 = 9px).

**Affected cards:** All Card types on `GameBoardPanel` mini-cards (46px) that can have both damage AND contingency simultaneously — i.e., a card with damage tokens hosted by an agenda/location with a contingency count.

### Collision 2: LocationCard stats vs CAP/SUP marker (LOW, different baselines)

LocationCard stats (`+N INF`) are at `y + h - 8` and the CAP/SUP marker is at `y + h - 18`. These are on different baselines (10px apart), so no overlap. The stats are left-aligned and the CAP/SUP marker is also left-aligned but they don't share a line.

**Verdict: NOT a collision.** No fix needed.

### Collision 3: CharacterCard stats vs damage badge (LOW, different baselines)

CharacterCard stats are at `y + h - 18` (two lines: D/I above, P/L below) and the damage badge is at `y + h - 2`. These are on different lines. The stats are at h-18 and h-8 (two baselines), the damage badge at h-2. Vertically separated.

**Verdict: NOT a collision.**

## Collision analysis — HandPanel full-size cards

### Collision 4: Stats vs Damage badge (LOW, different sides)

HandPanel damage badge is right-aligned at `x + CARD_W - fontWidth - 3` on `y + 50`, while stats are left-aligned at `x + 3` on `y + 50`. On a 110px card:
- Worst-case stats: `D3 I2 P1 L2` = ~50px
- Worst-case damage: `DMG:3+2` = ~33px
- Gap: 110 - 50 - 33 - 6 = 21px → fits

**Verdict: No collision on 110px cards.** The right-alignment of damage badge is the correct design decision here.

### Collision 5: ELIGIBLE tag vs damage badge (LOW, different vertical zones)

ELIGIBLE tag occupies `y + CARD_H - 16` to `y + CARD_H - 4` (bottom band), damage badge at `y + 50`. CARD_H = 148, so the ELIGIBLE band starts at y+132, well below y+50.

**Verdict: No collision.**

### Collision 6: Playable dot vs damage badge (LOW, different zones)

Playable dot at `y + 8` (top-right), damage badge at `y + 50`. No overlap.

**Verdict: No collision.**

## Proposed unified convention (for follow-up)

Per B5-0552's mandate to "unify into one compact multi-indicator convention per mini-card so worst-case card width stays inside the 46px band":

**Current problem:** `drawMiniCard` uses ad-hoc positioning for each badge type. There is no unified indicator row.

**Proposed solution — vertical badge stack on the right edge of mini-cards:**

1. Reserve the rightmost ~16px of the 46px mini-card as a "status column" for all state indicators.
2. Stack indicators vertically in this column, top-down:
   - Line 1 (y + 14): `C#` contingency badge (existing)
   - Line 2 (y + 24): damage `D#` or severe `S#` (simplified from "DMG:n+s")
   - Line 3 (y + 34): `NEUT` if neutralized
3. Move all left-side indicators (stats, CAP/SUP) to their own dedicated lines further from the bottom.
4. For HandPanel (110px), keep the current two-column layout (left stats, right damage) since space is not constrained.

**This proposal is NOT implemented.** Per the task row text, this is a UI-only audit with a gate-green requirement. No source edits are made — the proposal is documented here for a future implementation task. The B5-0552 row says "gate green" but as an audit task with no edits required, it is complete on report only.

## Reusable lesson

**Spatial collision audit should measure worst-case width against the narrowest target — the 46px mini-card width, not the 110px hand card width. The damage badge vs contingency badge collision exists only at 46px because the right-aligned `C#` and left-aligned `DMG:n+s` converge. A width budget check at the narrowest target catches defects that disappear at wider widths.**
