# B5-1109 — gate-versus-charge cost divergence on RECRUIT_CHARACTER

Agent: `Cline (space-bunny) b5-1109`. javac `1.8.0_292`. Scope: read-only
`engine/`, harness execution, one report, one pattern, one heartbeat. **No `src`
edit, no cost value changed, no card JSON changed, no fix made.**

## Verdict

**The divergence is real, and the row's second hypothesis is the correct one:
`canPlayCard` is not merely mispriced on this path — it is never consulted on it
at all.** The row asked which of two hypotheses held; the answer is *"a gate that
does not cover the path it appears to cover"*, not *"two disagreeing prices on a
path both gates read."*

`RulesEngine.canPlayCard` (`RulesEngine.java:904`) has **zero production call
sites**. Measured by grep over `b5ccg/src/**/*.java`:

| call site | file:line | production? |
|---|---|---|
| definition | `RulesEngine.java:904` | — |
| conformance test | `HeadlessConformanceTest.java:6166,6181,6183,6187,6188` | no (test) |
| headless seat probe | `HeadlessHumanSeatProbe.java:344,623-626` | no (harness) |

No `GameController`, `AIPlayer`, `MainWindow` or `HandPanel` line calls it. The
`RECRUIT_CHARACTER` branch (`GameController.java:220-250`) gates on
`rules.canRecruit` (line 226) and charges `rules.sponsorCost(...).getAmount()`
(line 231). The B5-1038 affordability gate simply is not on this path.

## The single correct cost notion

`RulesEngine.sponsorCost(p, ch).getAmount()` (`RulesEngine.java:242-247`) is the
one correct notion for sponsoring a character, and it is the notion both the real
gate (`canRecruit`, line 254) and the real charge (`GameController.java:231`)
already read. It composes, in order:

1. `baseRecruitCost` = `card.getCost()`, **doubled** when
   `isDoubleCostRequired` (other-race loyal, neutral/ANY exempt);
2. the assistant **sponsor discount** subtracted, floored at 0 (`recruitCost`);
3. a `FREE_SPONSOR` **waiver** collapsing the whole amount to 0.


## Measured divergences (scratch probe, since deleted; 9/9 pass)

Probe drove the real `GameController.processAction` by reflection against the
compiled `b5ccg/out` classes, so these are engine observations, not inferences.

| # | setup | `canPlayCard` | `canRecruit` | charge | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | NARN, own-race cost-3 char, pool 6 | true | true | 3 | agree |
| A2 | NARN, MINBARI cost-3 char, pool 6 | **false** | true | **6** | `canPlayCard` false on **faction**, not cost |
| B | NARN, own-race cost-3, discount 2, pool 2 | **false** | **true** | **1** | `canPlayCard` reads raw 3, gate reads 1 |
| B2 | NARN, MINBARI cost-3, pool 5 | false | false | 6 | `canRecruit` correctly refuses at the doubled 6 |
| C | RECRUIT dispatch, MINBARI cost-3, pool 6 | false | true | 6 | **charges doubled 6, seats the card** |
| D | PLAY_CARD dispatch, own-race cost-3, pool 6 | true | n/a | **3** | **charges RAW 3 and DISCARDS the card** |
| E | RECRUIT dispatch, MINBARI cost-3, pool 2 | false | false | 6 | refused, card **stays in hand** |
| F | unaffordable cost-5 enhancement, pool 1 | false | n/a | 5 | refused, card stays in hand |

Three things fall out of this table.

**(1) The recruit path is internally consistent.** C and E show `RECRUIT_CHARACTER`
gates and charges on the same `sponsorCost` notion, refusing cleanly and leaving
the card in hand when short. There is **no** "legal-looking move that does not
happen" on the recruit path. The row's stated player-facing risk does not
reproduce there.

**(2) `canPlayCard` is dead code, and its price is wrong in the *discount*
direction, not the doubling direction.** B is the case worth naming: with an
assistant sponsor discount of 2, the true charge is 1 while `canPlayCard` demands
raw cost 3, so a player with pool 2 is refused a recruit they can legitimately
pay for. That is the mirror image of the row's hypothesis — `canPlayCard` is
*stricter*, not more permissive, once the discount applies. It is currently
harmless only because nothing calls it.

**(3) The genuinely reachable defect is on the generic `PLAY_CARD` path, not the
recruit path (D).** `applyGenericCardPlay` (`GameController.java:630-684`) charges
`card.getCost()` raw and, for a card that is not an Enhancement/Location/Group/
Agenda/Event, falls through to `p.getDeck().discard(card)`. So dispatching
`playCard` on a **character** charges 3 instead of the sponsor 6, removes it from
hand, and **discards it — the character never enters the supporting role**. That
is a real state-corrupting path, and it is a *third* cost notion (raw cost) that
neither `canRecruit` nor the row's hypothesis named.

Reachability of D is bounded, and I did not claim more than I measured: the AI
offer loop (`AIPlayer.java:191-194`) routes characters to `canRecruit` +
`recruitCharacter` and never builds `playCard` for one; `HandPanel.cardPlayable`
(`HandPanel.java:184-198`) routes characters to `canRecruit`/`canPromote`; and
`MainWindow.playOnly` (`MainWindow.java:2133-2151`) refuses only `ConflictCard`,
so a selected in-hand character **can** reach `GameAction.playCard` through the
"Play Card" button, whose enable predicate (`MainWindow.java:2125`) does not
exclude characters. I did not execute the Swing path, so D is proven at the
`processAction` level and its UI reachability is read from source, not measured.

## What I did not do, and what I recommend

Per the row: no fix. My recommendation, for whoever claims it:

* **Do not** "fix" `canPlayCard` to read `sponsorCost`. It is a generic
  play-card predicate over `Card`, not a character predicate; a discount-aware
  cost belongs in `canRecruit`, which already has it. Widening `canPlayCard`
  would entrench dead code.
* **Delete `canPlayCard`, or wire it in.** Today it is a predicate with no
  production caller, which is the worst of both: it looks like a gate, it is
  exercised by the conformance suite, and it protects nothing.
* **The real fix is on `PLAY_CARD`.** Either refuse a `CharacterCard` in
  `applyGenericCardPlay` (route it to the sponsor branch), or exclude characters
  from the generic play path in `MainWindow.playOnly` and in the "Play Card"
  enable predicate. Until then a character dispatched as a generic play is
  charged raw and thrown into the discard pile.
* **The `FREE_SPONSOR` waiver is currently vacuous.** `CardEffects.WAIVER_EFFECTS`
  populates only `FREE_PARTICIPANT` for `conf_non_aligned_support` and its
  deluxe twin; `sponsorWaiver` maps every card to `NONE`. The waiver third of
  the cost composition is correct code over an empty set, so no character can be
  free-sponsored today.

## Reusable lesson

**A gate that nothing calls is not a mispriced gate — it is a gate-shaped hole,
so measure the call sites before comparing prices: enumerate who invokes the
predicate, because a divergence "between a gate and a charge" is only meaningful
once both are on the same path.**
