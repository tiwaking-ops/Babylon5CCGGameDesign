# B5-0366 — AI rotate-for-effect offers + scoring

## Summary
Completed the AI side of B5-0366 (B5-0345 Tier-2 #5): `AIPlayer` now offers
`USE_ROTATE_EFFECT` actions for every legal (assistant, ambassador, effect-kind)
triple, and scores them in MEDIUM and HARD. The engine/model/controller sides
were already implemented (GameAction factory + RotateEffectKind enum, RulesEngine
canUseRotateEffect/executeRotateEffect, GameController USE_ROTATE_EFFECT branch,
HeadlessConformanceTest ROT + ROT-C sections).

## Changes (2 files, AI + conformance only)

### `b5ccg/src/b5ccg/ai/AIPlayer.java` (+29/−0)
- **`buildLegalActions`**: after the LEAD_FLEET section, iterate over each
  supporting-role character + the player's ambassador; for each ready
  (unrotated, face-up) assistant offer both `RotateEffectKind` values
  (`USE_ABILITY_BOOST`, `USE_SPONSOR_DISCOUNT`) gated by
  `rules.canUseRotateEffect` (delegates to `canUseAssistant` — same readiness
  as B5-0339). No offer when the assistant is rotated or the ambassador is null.
- **`scoreActionMedium`**: `USE_ROTATE_EFFECT` scores 2 for the ability boost
  (+1 Diplomacy/Intrigue/Leadership on the ambassador while rotated) and 1 for
  the sponsor discount (saves 1 influence on the next recruit). Below promote /
  build-influence peaks so the AI leads with Inner Circle growth first.
- **`scoreActionHard`**: same two kinds, scored 1.0 (boost) and 0.5 (discount).
  Deterministic, no RNG — list-order tie-breaking consistent with every other
  action type.

### `b5ccg/src/b5ccg/engine/HeadlessConformanceTest.java` (+63/−0)
New `testRotateAI()` section (ROT-AI, 5 checks):
1. MEDIUM offers a rotate-effect for a ready assistant (boost or discount).
2. MEDIUM picks the ability boost over the sponsor discount (score 2 > 1).
3. HARD offers and picks the boost (score 1.0 > 0.5).
4. EASY offers the rotate-effect (shares the same legal-action builder) and
   picks it sometimes over 40 runs — offer-level, not scoring.
5. No tier offers rotate-effect for a rotated assistant (deterministic absence).
