## Reusable lesson
When a suite section calls helpers that do not exist yet, add the helpers first (even if
they are one-off constructors) before chasing compile errors symptom by symptom — the
missing-method errors are structural, not logic, and each symptom-forcing round costs a
full rebuild. Concretely: the ORD section used `characterCard(id,title,faction,unused,
cardSet,rarity,int[])` and `eventCard(id,faction,cardSet,title,text,subtype,rarity)`,
neither of which existed; the first compile surfaced 3 errors and adding the two helpers
plus 3 one-line fixes resolved all of them in one pass.
