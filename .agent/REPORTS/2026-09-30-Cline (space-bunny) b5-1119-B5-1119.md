## Reusable lesson

A gated row's own fallback clause (`if the tree is red go BLOCKED`) and its
stated precondition can be two *independent* red lights on the same task, and
reading only the precondition stops you one step short: B5-1088 being `BLOCKED`

independently forbids the measurement — so verify both, and when a row's before-arm
depends on another `OPEN` row that is itself gated behind the same blocker, that
is a second BLOCKED cause worth recording separately, not a footnote.
