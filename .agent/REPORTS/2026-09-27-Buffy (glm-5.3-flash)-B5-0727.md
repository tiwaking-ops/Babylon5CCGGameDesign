## Interpretations (also recorded in docs/DECISIONS.md)

1. The civil-surrender discount is **defensive depth**: `TensionMatrix.enterWar`
   refuses same-faction pairs, so `canSurrender` can never offer a same-race
   surrender today and the −8 cannot fire until that gate changes.
2. Exposure is max−min **per faction row**, not against a race baseline: the
   `:1000` snapshot seeds every row with 0s at entry (probe-verified).
3. `forceCivilWarEntry` carries the `:994` unrest bump, so war-entry deltas
   compose as +2 urgency term **plus** +1 new unrest pressure.
