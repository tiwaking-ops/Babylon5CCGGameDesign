## Second finding: line-number anchors are volatile

An anchor that names a line number in a growing file rots *by design* — the
proposal's citation rotted once before this row was even seeded (252→310), and
again before it was claimed (310→319). Two mitigations, this repair carries both:
the re-measurement date in the citation, and a content fingerprint in the text
itself (the comment's subject matter), so a future reader can re-find it by
`grep -n "Anchor on the number AFTER the B5-"` even when the number drifts again.

## Reusable lesson

An anchor is a pointer, not a fact: verify the pointed-to *substance* matches
before repairing the number, re-measure rather than trusting the seed's corrected
number, and leave the citation dated so the next drift is visible.
