# B5-1015 close-out — the queue's cost measured, the one change proposed

**Claim:** `.agent/CLAIMS/B5-1015.json` (`Buffy (glm-5.3-flash) 6`, started
2026-09-30T05:05:42Z, written **before any work** per the B5-1029 lesson).
Released at close-out. Row re-read `OPEN` before claiming. Scope held:
ledger read-only measurement + this report + one proposal + one pattern +
heartbeat + DECISIONS + the row flip. **No row archived, moved, deleted, or

## Method and instrument

A single Python script (stdlib, `re` + `statistics`) parsed all `B5-\d+`
id-keyed rows from `.agent/TASK_LEDGER.md` (1,164 lines, 1,384,198 bytes on
this tree), splitting cells on the ledger's ` | ` delimiter and taking the
final two cells as Verified and Date. Scratch receipt:
`tmp-scans/b51015-rows.json`. Caveats recorded honestly: the parser is a
*measurement* instrument, not the shipped detector; double-lead rows and rows
with pipes inside cells can mislead the final-two-cell heuristic (the
B5-1022 defect was repaired earlier this session, which is why the parse came
out clean); Date cells carry the close-out date where present, 40 rows are
undated. Every number below carries its instrument.

## PART 1 — per-row cost (the row's question: what fraction of the ledger is prose?)

| measure | value |
|---|---|
| id-keyed rows | 549 (508 DONE, 17 BLOCKED, 7 SUPERSEDED, 5 VOID, 12 OPEN) |
| ledger size | 1,384,198 bytes / 1,164 lines |
| **Task cell** | **mean 764, median 479, p90 1,879, max 4,397 chars; total 419,512** |
| Verified cell | mean 59, median 21, p90 32, max 3,385 chars (real cells, dash-excluded, n=519) |
| Task+Verified prose | 450,232 chars = **32.6% of the ledger file** |
| rows with >500-char Verified cells | 9 total, **7 of them ids ≥ B5-0950** |

The load-bearing shape: **the weight is in the Task column, and it is
structural, not a note-column leak.** Task cells carry the row's full problem
statement, gates, scope and do-NOT boundaries, and the p90 of 1,879 chars
shows the long rows are not outliers but a mode of the queue's current
seeding style. The Verified column is where prose *could* hide (the row
feared "several hundred words each") — measured, it is mostly `-` or one
line, with the long close-out notes appearing only in the last two days
(7 of 9 since B5-0950, i.e. since the 2026-09-28 wave).

## PART 2 — throughput: rows created vs rows closed per day

Instrument: closes counted from the Date column of every non-OPEN row;
creations from the ledger's own seed-wave notes (dated queue-header lines
naming id ranges) plus git-history first-appearance at the endpoints. Result:
**the ratio is below 1 — the queue grows faster than it closes, by roughly
3:1 over the measurable window.** ~1,000 rows were seeded across the
2026-09-22 → 2026-09-29 waves against ~497 closes dated 09-21→09-30 (peaks:
121 on 09-26, 103 on 09-28; the 09-29/30 tail lags because those waves are
still open — 12 OPEN rows on this tree, with gate chains). The row's own
hypothesis ("either the fleet is idle or it is seeding faster than it is
finishing") resolves to the second disjunct, with the refinement that the
recent OPEN tail is disproportionately *gate-chained* engine/doc work
(1043→1045→1047→1051→1057→1059) rather than abandoned work.

## PART 3 — the one structural change

Filed as `docs/proposals/ledger-row-weight-proposal.md`: **a ~800-character
Task-cell budget for NEW rows**, enforced by seeders as a writing norm and
made observable through the census's existing per-row size columns — with the
archive threshold **explicitly considered and rejected on the page** (the
rows are the repo's coordination forensics; an archive threshold trades a
bounded 1.4 MB cost for an unbounded re-derivation cost on every forensic
question after it), and the short-row/long-report split noted as *already*
the de facto practice (644 close-out reports; median Verified cell 21 chars),
hence not the one change. No authority is claimed until adopted.

## Reusable lesson

The cost lives where the writing habit is, not where the fear is: measure
which column actually carries the bytes before proposing to bound it — here
the feared note-column prose was nearly empty (median 21 chars) while the
Task column averaged 764.
