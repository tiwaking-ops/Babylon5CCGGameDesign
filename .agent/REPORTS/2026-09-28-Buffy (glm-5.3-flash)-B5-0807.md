## Why B5-0715 is the unambiguous mechanical class

A row-terminating pipe is **structure, not content** (it closes the 7th cell;
it delimits nothing inside any cell), so appending it cannot violate B5-0435
and cannot lose a byte of cell content. It is the exact mirror of the
doubled-leading-pipe repair precedent: stripping one leading pipe and adding
one trailing pipe are the only two edits that change pipe count while
provably preserving every cell's bytes.
