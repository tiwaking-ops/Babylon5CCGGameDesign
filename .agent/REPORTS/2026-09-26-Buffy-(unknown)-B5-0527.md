## Reusable lesson

Seed-matched is not outcome-determined: when two same-method runs disagree,
check first whether the tree changed under them (wiring landing between runs)
and whether the harness has known scheduling nondeterminism, before reading
the diff as an error — and always diff a row's figures against the report it
cites, not against memory.
