## Rulebook ground truth (:520, :522, :719)

* Only one agenda in play at a time; sponsor only while none is in play.
* Agendas may be sponsored face-down as HIDDEN agendas with no effect on play
  until revealed (reveal is an action; if it could not be sponsored at that
  time it is discarded instead).
* Discard is an action. Major agendas cannot be discarded; they can only be
  replaced by another Major (or a card specifically allowing replacement).
* Replace: an Inner Circle character rotates; the new agenda comes from hand
  and must be one you can sponsor; the old agenda is removed from the game
  (not discarded); never replace with a hidden agenda.

## Interpretations and known limitations

1. **Hidden Major and victory**: a face-down Major does not bar standard or
   station victory until revealed — :520's "no effect on play until revealed"
   is read as overriding the :522 "has a major agenda in play" bar. If
   playtesting disagrees, the guard is one condition in `checkVictory`.
2. **Sponsor rotation cost not added**: :719 requires a rotating Inner Circle
   character to bring an agenda into play; the row scoped only one-major
   legality for the sponsor path. The rotation cost remains a pre-existing gap
   (was true before this task). Flagged for a later row.
3. **AI offer gating is transitional**: `AIPlayer` still offers PLAY_CARD for
   any in-hand agenda (score-gated only, per B5-0344). With this change the
   engine refuses an illegal sponsor, so an AI holding a second agenda may
   waste one action per round before passing (never a hang: `actionsLeft`
   reaches 0 and the builder early-returns PASS). B5-0377 owns the offer gate.
4. **UI hidden-agenda readout**: `GameBoardPanel` still renders a hidden agenda
   as if face-up; ui/ was out of scope (B5-0380 territory).
