## 6. A mistake I made and repaired

My first write of the ledger row used PowerShell's `-join '\|'`. `-join` takes its
separator **literally**, unlike `-split`'s regex, so it wrote `\|` before every
pipe and added a spurious 8th. I caught it because the post-write pipe check read
`8` instead of `7`, diagnosed the cause rather than normalising blindly, repaired
the one line I had touched, and confirmed the only other backslash-pipe rows
