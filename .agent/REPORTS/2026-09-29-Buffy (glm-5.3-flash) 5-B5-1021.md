# B5-1021 - The four unannotated deluxe/premiere text divergences

Report-only. Instruments named: python3 json.load over both card files with the
same title-join `DeckLoader` dedup uses, whitespace-normalisation control, grep
over `CardEffects.java` for engine dispatch, and the pool-baseline report's field
histogram. No JSON edited, no annotation written, no src touched.

## Reproduction of the census, with denominators stated

My title-join over all 383 deluxe records against premiere-by-title finds **154**
text divergences, of which **150** carry the `(Deluxe ...)` parenthetical and
**exactly 4** do not — and the 4 are precisely `de_fleet_drazi_sunhawk`,
`de_agenda_as_it_was_meant_to_be`, `de_enh_prophecy`, `de_group_spin_doctors`,
the same four the twin census of record found. My 154 differs from the census's
128 because the denominators differ (all title-joined deluxe records vs the
census's 325 twin pairs); both readings agree on the load-bearing finding, and
neither overwrites the other. Whitespace-normalisation changes nothing (all 154
are real content differences).

## The four, verbatim, with adjudication

### 1. `de_fleet_drazi_sunhawk` / "Drazi Sunhawk" (FLEET, NON_ALIGNED, cost 4, military 3 — all stats identical)

- **Premiere:** "Drazi warships. May be played by any faction. While in play, this faction's Military conflicts gain +1."
- **Deluxe:** "Drazi warships. May be played by any faction."

The deluxe silently **removes a persistent bonus**, with no compensating change
(cost and military identical) and no annotation. Shape is the first two sentences
kept and the third dropped at a sentence boundary. **UNDECIDABLE, leaning
DATA-ERROR (truncation)**: 150/154 deliberate changes in this dataset are
*annotated*; a silent removal of a bonus with zero rebalance elsewhere is not a
design direction any annotated change in the file follows. **What would decide
it:** the rendered deluxe Sunhawk card (or an out-of-repo deluxe card list) —
if the printed card drops the While-in-play line it is a deliberate erratum; if
it keeps it, the JSON lost a sentence in transcription. Engine impact today:
zero either way — no structured field carries the +1, and the card has no
CardEffects dispatch.

### 2. `de_agenda_as_it_was_meant_to_be` / "As It Was Meant To Be" (AGENDA_MAJOR; rarity RARE_WITHDRAWN vs RARE)

- **Premiere:** "Major Agenda. Win Condition: Reach 20 Influence. Ongoing: Once per round, you may rotate this Agenda to force all other players to pass their next action."
- **Deluxe:** "[WITHDRAWN – not legal for tournament play.] Major Agenda. Win Condition: Reach 20 Influence. Ongoing: Once per round, rotate this Agenda to force all other players to pass their next action."

Two distinct divergences. **(a) The withdrawal is DELIBERATE** — marked twice,
in the rarity field (the enum's only `RARE_WITHDRAWN` record) and in the bracket
prefix; a lifecycle state, not a balance value, exactly as the census of record
held. **(b) "you may rotate" → "rotate": UNDECIDABLE but low-stakes** — in this
dataset's own templating ("rotate this X to do Y", e.g. Prophecy, Spin Doctors)
the rotate is the activation cost, so the deluxe imperative most plausibly reads
as the same activated ability with tightened wording; a mandatory
auto-pass-forcing reading cannot be excluded from the data alone. **What would
decide it:** the printed deluxe card text, plus a human ruling if the printed
card really drops "you may". No engine divergence is possible today: no agenda
dispatch consumes this wording (effects are prose-only).

### 3. `de_enh_prophecy` / "Prophecy" (ENHANCEMENT_CHARACTER, cost 2, psiBonus 1 — **both** files)

- **Premiere:** "Attach to a Character. Once per game, rotate this Enhancement to look at the top 5 cards of any draw pile and rearrange them in any order."
- **Deluxe:** same + "That character also gains +1 Psi."

**DELIBERATE, mechanically neutral, annotation merely missing — the only one of
the four the structured data refutes as a divergence:** `psiBonus` reads **1 in
both editions**, so the +1 Psi the deluxe prose adds already existed
mechanically in premiere. The deluxe text is playing catch-up with the stats,
not changing the design; the engine has implemented the +1 identically in both
editions all along (via the structured field, not the prose). Contrast with the
annotated `de_enh_latent_telepath`, where the deluxe change (psiBonus 2→3) is a
real rebalance *and* is annotated. **What would confirm:** the printed deluxe
card (expected to carry the extra sentence); but no rules consequence hangs on
it either way.

### 4. `de_group_spin_doctors` / "Spin Doctors" (GROUP, cost 8, RARE — all stats identical)

- **Premiere:** "Rotate this Group after any conflict. Change the perceived winner of a Diplomacy conflict for Influence gain purposes (you gain their Influence; they gain none)."
- **Deluxe:** same, minus the parenthetical.

The deluxe **removes a clarifying parenthetical**, making the text strictly
vaguer (who gains the Influence?) without obviously changing the rule. **UNDECIDABLE, leaning DATA-ERROR (lossy transcription)**: this dataset's deliberate
changes *add or reword* clauses and carry annotations; deletions that reduce
clarity with no annotation fit an accident, not a design pass. **What would
decide it:** the printed deluxe card. Engine impact today: zero — the
influence-steer effect is prose-only (no dispatch).

## Is the annotation convention real, and do the outliers cluster?

The convention is **real**: 150 of 154 divergent deluxe texts (97.4%) carry the
parenthetical, uniformly across card types (checked across the full join, not
sampled). The 4 outliers are one FLEET, one AGENDA, one ENHANCEMENT, one GROUP —
**no clustering in any card type**, which fits scattered hand-editing accidents
rather than a systematically different treatment of one type. A convention with
a 97.4% adoption rate and four scattered exceptions is a convention with four
bugs, not four policy zones.

## Reusable lesson

Structured fields can refute a text divergence: check the twin's stats before
calling a prose difference a rules change — Prophecy's "+1 Psi" sentence
describes a bonus both editions' data already grants, which converts an
apparent erratum into a harmless clarification at zero evidence cost.
