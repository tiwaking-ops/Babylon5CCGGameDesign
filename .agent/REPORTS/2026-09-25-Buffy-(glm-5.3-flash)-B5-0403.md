- `AIPlayer.buildLegalActions`: one bid offer per offered mercenary per
  eligibility visit — the minimal increment `bestOther - mine + 1` that puts
  the player STRICTLY ahead of the best competing cumulative total (B5-0395
  ties-crown-nobody discipline), gated by `rules.canBidOnMercenary`
  (pool affordability, afford-now). A player already strictly leading does
  not re-bid; if a rival overbids the offer reappears on a later visit

  offer list (difficulty contract untouched).
- `AIPlayer.scoreActionMedium` BID_ON_MERCENARY: `round(4.0 * winProb −
  inc * 0.5)` with winProb = (projected+1)/(projected+bestOther+2) — the
  same idiom as HARD initiation/LEAD_FLEET.
- `AIPlayer.scoreActionHard` BID_ON_MERCENARY: `5.0 * winProb − inc * 0.75`
  (steeper opportunity cost; pool-only spend per B5-0395 — Rating never
  touched).

  HARD outbid standing highest by exactly one; MEDIUM/HARD pass when the
