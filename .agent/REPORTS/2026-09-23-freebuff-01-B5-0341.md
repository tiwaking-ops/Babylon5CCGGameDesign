## Key findings recorded in the proposal

1. **The consecutive-pass loop already exists.** `GameController.runActionPhase`'s
   `passCount` reset loop is exactly the rulebook's end condition. The audit's
   "rework the pass-loop" undersells how close the structure is — the real
   defects are the `actionsLeft = 1` grant, the sticky passed-flag (a passer
   can never act again, contradicting §V Pass), and B5-0202c's early-PASS
   guard, which would deadlock an AI under unlimited eligibility.
2. **Hard ordering dependency on D9 (B5-0342).** Unlimited actions against
   the current influence semantics (permanent rating, no per-turn applied
   pool) would let a wealthy faction convert its entire rating in one round.
   The rulebook is only self-consistent because influence applied during a
   turn comes out of the per-turn pool (§Influence). Recommendation:
   implement B5-0342 first or combined, never D6 alone.
3. **Termination argument.** Every non-pass action consumes a
   round-renewable resource (influence, ready cards, once-per-turn conflict
   marker, rotations), so MEDIUM/HARD AIs with floored, cost-aware scoring
   self-terminate; a non-rulebook safety cap (`8 × playerCount`, loud log)
   guards harness liveness against pathological behavior.
4. Un-passing is a one-line semantic (`setPassed(false)` on any non-pass
   action) with outsized rulebook impact.
5. Observable changes quantified: the deterministic 8-action round profile
   (B5-0312) becomes variable; suite sections are action-economy-independent
   and stay valid; acceptance criteria (6) and risks (4) are enumerated for
   the implementation task.
