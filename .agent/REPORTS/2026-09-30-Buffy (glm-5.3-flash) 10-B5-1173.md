## Headline

**Zero of 829 card records mention "sponsor" or "waiv" in their text.** The data
contains no free-sponsor promise at all, so the vacuous `FREE_SPONSOR` wiring
B5-1109 named is not a broken promise — it is a **provision** for a mechanic the
transcription never carried. The correct reading of
`CardEffects.WAIVER_EFFECTS` holding no `FREE_SPONSOR` entry is "unused
headroom", not "lost effect".

## Two receipts for whoever wires the waiver later

1. **The dedupe trap.** The premiere `conf_non_aligned_support` id is **absent
   from the pool** — the title-dedupe keeps the `de_` twin — so the
   `FREE_PARTICIPANT` wiring rides entirely on the `de_` id. Anyone adding
   waiver entries by premiere id wires a dead key. (Corroborates the B5-1147
   armistice-twins finding from the loader side.)
2. **The rulebook semantics gap.** Rulebook §Free (:1154) defines
   "sponsor for free" as: immediately, any round, no influence cost, **without
   rotating a sponsoring character**. `sponsorWaiver` can only express the
   cost half; a future `FREE_SPONSOR` wiring must land the immediacy and
   no-rotate clauses too, or it will silently under-implement the rulebook.

## Disjointness

This is a **waiver** census: disjoint from B5-1125 (id-keyed **registration**
census) and from B5-1153 (**trigger**-keyed aftermath census) by construction,
as the seed letter required.

## Reusable lesson

**A vacuous wiring is a provision: before calling dead code a defect, measure
what the data promises — an empty table over zero promises is headroom, an
empty table over kept promises is a loss, and only the census tells you which.**
