# B5-0368 — damage and neutralization model

Added per-card normal and severe damage counters. Damage reduces every printed/effective ability by its normal damage count, floored at zero. Reaching the current greatest ability neutralizes a card, removes the normal tokens required for neutralization, and converts excess into severe damage. Additional normal damage to a neutralized card has no effect. Bonus-aware greatest-ability reads reconcile neutralization when stat bonuses change.

Fleet neutralization also neutralizes its rotated leader without transferring damage or severe damage. Cards neutralized during a turn remain action-locked even if healed; the lock clears at the next `RulesEngine.startRound()`. Locations now load and expose their printed Military value for damage-aware conflict reads. The existing B5-0309 ambassador flip remains token-free and face-down as before.

Added seven DMG conformance checks for ability reduction/flooring, overflow conversion, no further normal damage, action lock/reset, fleet leader neutralization, and the legacy B5-0309 path.
