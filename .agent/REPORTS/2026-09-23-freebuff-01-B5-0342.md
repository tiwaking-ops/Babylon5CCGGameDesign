## Key findings recorded in the proposal

1. **The conflation is live behavior, not a latent gap.** The audit's D9
   predates the B5-0321/B5-0323 spend wiring; today every sponsor/promote
   permanently reduces Power, and Build Influence — the action whose purpose
   is raising the Rating — nets −2 permanent Rating per +1 (the engine's own
   comment reasons in applied-pool terms: "net pool change = −2"). The
   rulebook intends pool −3 for the turn, Rating +1 permanently.
2. **Additive design, no signature scramble.** `influence` keeps its name
   and Rating meaning (victory, AI scoring, agenda conditions, UI unchanged);
   a new `appliedPool` with apply/restore semantics carries the spendable
   portion; `restoreAppliedPool()` at `startRound` implements the rulebook's
   turn restoration.
3. **68-site census mapped** (grep across model/engine/ai): effects stay
   permanent (D9.3), the four spend sites migrate to the pool, affordability
   gates switch to the pool, victory keeps reading the Rating.
4. **Six derived rules** (D9.1–D9.6) extracted from §Influence, including
   the mid-turn Rating-change rule (a permanent gain is immediately
   spendable; a loss clamps the pool) and Build Influence's dual check.
5. **Suite migration callout:** the CST/PRM/AST spend-amount assertions must
   move to pool semantics in the same change or the gate goes red.
6. **Ordering:** this slice precedes (or combines with) the D6
   unlimited-actions implementation — B5-0341's termination argument relies
   on the finite per-turn pool.
