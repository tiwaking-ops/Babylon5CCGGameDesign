# B5-0363 — Human conflict-join window

`GameController` now offers a side-choice window during conflict resolution after AI seats decide and before mandatory participation and resolution. If the game has a UI callback and a non-forfeited human non-initiator can legally join under both action and participation checks, the controller waits for a side choice. Existing `JOIN_CONFLICT_SUPPORT` and `JOIN_CONFLICT_OPPOSE` submissions route to this wait, add the participant and face-up ambassador through `RulesEngine.executeJoinConflict`, and wake the controller. Non-join actions and stale/double join submissions do not leak into the next action turn.

`MainWindow` enables Support/Oppose only during that eligible window and displays a prompt. `docs/playtest-guide.md` §§3–4 now describe the decision window and the remaining mandatory-participation behavior. AI join decisions, mandatory enforcement, resolution outcomes, and AI aftermath auto-play retain their order.
