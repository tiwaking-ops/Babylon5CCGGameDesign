# B5-0358 — War-conflict participation rules

**Deliverable:** `docs/proposals/war-conflict-participation-rules-proposal.md`.
Proposal-only task; zero edits to `b5ccg/src/` or `b5ccg/resources/`.

## What was delivered

A complete design for war-conflict participation over the three missing
layers, from the rulebook's §War (:799–815) plus the :374 targeting sample:

1. **State.** Engine-owned `TensionMatrix`: pairwise tension 0–5 plus an
   explicit `atWar` set with named entry points — deliberately **not**
   `tension == 5` derivation, because the rulebook's war entry triggers are
   card- and effect-driven. Recorded interpretation: the resolution-side
   tension increment does not itself declare war. Not in JSON (engine-owned
   per the task row; card data would only ever carry triggers).
2. **Action.** `DECLARE_WAR_CONFLICT` with no hand/influence check
   (:805 verbatim), consuming the one-conflict-per-turn slot (B5-0302
   preserved); the race-vs-location target is named at initiation per the
   :374 sample — mirroring B5-0336's declare/reveal split.
3. **Outcome.** Race-target wars resolve through the rulebook's
   uncontested test — all-supported read directly from the B5-0309 side
   sets; the "or attacked" half is a documented interim constant until
   B5-0345 Tier-3 attacks exist. Win + uncontested ⇒ −1/+1 influence
   swing; win + contested ⇒ no effect (but aftermath-eligible Won).
   Location-target wars capture on win: `capturedBy` +
   `effectsSuppressed` gate income and Military, enhancements are
   discarded, recapture restores. Tension rises +1 toward the initiator
   after every war resolution, clamped at 5.

Also recorded: same-race non-aggression (:974) and civil war as record-only
(single faction per race today; the matrix is keyed on faction pairs for
future), the synthetic-ConflictCard rejection (a fake card id would poison
every `card.getId()`-keyed CardEffects table), and 3 phases + 8 conformance
tests with named risks.
