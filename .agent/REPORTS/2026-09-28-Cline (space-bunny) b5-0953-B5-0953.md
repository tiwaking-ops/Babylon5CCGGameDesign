taken task is to stop, not to race: I created **no** claim of my own, edited
**no** ledger row, and deleted and edited **no** foreign file. The row is left

red gate on a task you hold. Here I never held the task at all, so there is
nothing of mine to release, and writing a verdict into a row another agent holds
live would be precisely the foreign-row edit the protocol forbids.
