# B5-0396 — Deluxe Commercial Telepaths text hygiene

## Assessment

B5-0385 already rewrote the **Premiere** `group_commercial_telepaths` text to
an IP-safe paraphrase after B5-0355's audit found it was the only card at 96%
word overlap with the copyrighted SNRPG database text. The **Deluxe** reprint
`de_group_commercial_telepaths` was left untouched in B5-0385, flagged for a
dedicated follow-up ("much lower overlap, left untouched").

The Deluxe text was:

> "Rotate this Group and target a Character. That Character gains a bonus to
> their Diplomacy equal to half their Psi (rounded up) while this Group
> remains rotated. (Deluxe art/text change: may now target any character,
> including opponents'.)"

While the Deluxe wording differs from the Premiere version (and from the 96%-overlap
outlier), B5-0388's operating rule #5 directs handling isolated IP-safety fixes
independently. The Deluxe text still uses "gains a bonus to their Diplomacy equal
to half their Psi" phrasing that could carry residual IP risk. Per the policy, the
same paraphrase style was applied for safety and consistency.

## Change

Rewrote the Deluxe `text` field in `b5ccg/resources/cards/deluxe.json` to:

> "Rotate this Group and choose a Character. For as long as this Group stays
> rotated, that Character adds half its Psi (rounded up) to its Diplomacy.
> (Deluxe rules change: may choose any Character, including opponents'.)"

Engine hook tokens preserved byte-stable: `rotate`, `Character`, `Psi`, `Diplomacy`.
The Deluxe-specific gameplay change (targeting any character, including opponents)
is preserved in the parenthetical, rephrased from "art/text change" to "rules change"
for clarity. Values only — no stats, costs, or other fields touched.
