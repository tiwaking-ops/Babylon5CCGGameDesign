## Root causes (both in the concurrent uncommitted MINES fixture edits)

1. **Trailing-period needles (3 checks).** The fixture expected
   `"N damage; M damage returned."` but the engine's fixed format is
   `"...returned)." ` — the period sits after the closing paren, so a needle
   ending `returned."` can never substring-match. The committed fixture had
   no trailing periods (0527-era formatting); the concurrent edit added them.
2. **Stale pre-rotation in the round-trip (2 checks).** The fixture called
   `rtAttCard.rotate()` before `executeAttackConflictParticipant`; the gate
   requires a ready (unrotated) attacker, so the attack never ran and no log
   entry existed for either check. The rotation predates the B5-0366-era
   gate shape — the committed scenario could not have passed since.

## Fixes (HeadlessConformanceTest.java MINES section only, 4 hunks)

* Needle period fixes: `"2 damage; 4 damage returned."` → `"… returned"`,
  `"1 damage; 3 damage returned."` → `"… returned"`,

## Reusable lesson

fire, print the exact bytes of the actual and expected strings before
touching logic — invisible trailing punctuation (`.` vs `)` beats `.`) is a
cheaper root cause than a broken hook, and needle/format drift between a
hand-edited fixture and a fixed log format is a recurring failure class.**
