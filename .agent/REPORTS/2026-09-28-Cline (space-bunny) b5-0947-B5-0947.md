## Reusable lesson

A dependency gate written as "claim only after X…Y are all DONE" can be
satisfied by seven of eight and still be **red**, and a task whose deliverable
is a *census* must not publish a partial one: because status is keyed by task
ID, a partial census published `DONE` is indistinguishable from a complete one
to every later reader. **Measure the gate's full conjunction before claiming,
and treat a missing member as a blocker rather than as a reason to widen scope
and do the missing member yourself.**
