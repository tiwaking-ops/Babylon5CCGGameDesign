## What landed

A damage-state readout block in `GameBoardPanel.drawMiniCard`
(inserted after the B5-0381 contingency badge, ~24 lines incl. comments):

* **Source of truth:** the B5-0368 Card base-class API —
  `getDamageTokens()`, `getSevereDamageTokens()`, `isNeutralized()`
  (model/Card.java:78-80) — so **every** mini-card qualifies: Inner
  Circle characters, supporting characters, fleets, groups, locations,
  and the large ambassador card. One insertion point, no per-type
  branches (the B5-0504 audit's "zero damage markers on any surface" gap
  is closed for all card types at once).
* **Face-up only**, per the B5-0427 captured/suppressed readout
  precedent: a face-down card's state stays as opaque as its identity
  (B5-0381 discipline).
* **Compact marker form**, red (255,80,80) bold 7pt at the card's bottom
  baseline (x+2, y+h−2):
  - damage present: `DMG:<n>` (e.g. `DMG:2`), with severe appended as
    `+<s>` (e.g. `DMG:2+1`);
  - neutralized with damage: `NEUT <n>+<s>` (neutralized at zero damage:
    `NEUT`);
  - nothing shown when undamaged and un-neutralized.
* The right-aligned `C<count>` contingency badge (B5-0381) shares the
  baseline; the compact form keeps worst-case combined width within the
  46px card. Known corner (honest note): a small card showing
  neutralized + severe damage + a contingency badge simultaneously can
  overlap the two badges by ~1px — cosmetic only, and the rarest
  combination on the board.

## Reusable lesson

Readout slices that read a base-class API should insert at the shared
rendering point, not per card type — one insertion gives every current
and future card type the marker for free, and per-type branches would
have had to be re-added for each new zone anyway.
