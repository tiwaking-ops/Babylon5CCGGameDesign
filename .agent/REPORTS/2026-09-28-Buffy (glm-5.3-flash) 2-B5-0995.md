outside PREMIERE/DELUXE (census set-split sums to 77 of 78)". Re-reading the

B5-0980 report (37+41 mis-summed as 77), then paraphrased into the DECISIONS
entry, then **seeded forward by me into this very row** — a citation-decay loop
where the decay was authored by the same session that later caught it. The
B5-0980 report, its DECISIONS entry, and the B5-0995 seed text carry the error
forward on disk; this report and its DECISIONS entry are the erratum of record
