# B5-0703 — AI Power awareness (close-out)

## What changed

Three position-evaluation sites in `AIPlayer.java` switched from `getInfluence()` to `getPower()`:

1. **`agendaProximityScore`** (line 1044): INFLUENCE_20 agendas evaluate Power, not Influence — `p.getInfluence() / 2` → `p.getPower() / 2`.
2. **`eventCatchUpBonus`** (lines 1076-1079): the trailing-player catch-up term now measures Power gaps — both best-other scan and self-threshold use `getPower()`.
3. **`leadingPlayer`** (line 1087): the leader the AI targets/avoids is now the Power leader — `p.getInfluence()` → `p.getPower()`.

These are the only three sites where the AI evaluates *who is ahead* for scoring purposes. The surrender threshold (B5-0679), station context (B5-0453), build-influence cap (rulebook VI ≤9), and major-victory proximity (B5-0635) all stay on `getInfluence()` — they are influence-economy concerns, not Power position, and the B5-0667 ruling + B5-0701 report record that switching them would be a no-op today (no Power-bearing card in the pool) and a half-applied split tomorrow.

EASY never calls any of these three helpers (it uses `easyChoose` uniformly), so the EASY difficulty contract (B5-0351) is untouched.

## Zero-cost invariance

`Player.getPower()` (B5-0677) is defined as `getInfluence() + POWER-tagged StatBonus total`. With no POWER bonuses in the pool (measured vacuum, B5-0667), `getPower() == getInfluence()` for every player on every turn. Therefore today's AI ordering is byte-identical to pre-B5-0703: every score, every pick, every leader computation returns the same value. The mechanism goes live only when a POWER-bearing card exists — which is data-gated per B5-0667.

Verification: the three patched lines are the only `getPower()` calls that feed position evaluation in ai/. The remaining `getInfluence()` calls in AIPlayer.java (lines 153, 405, 468, 480, 519, 538, 542, 559, 563, 653, 846) are all in the scope-excluded categories above.

## Reusable lesson

Position-evaluation seams (who is ahead) and economy seams (how much resource) are different concerns even when they read the same number today — converting only the position reads to a computed Power keeps the economy layer on Influence per the ruling, preserves zero-cost invariance automatically, and avoids the half-applied split the B5-0667 ruling warned against. When a seam is defined as "computed, never stored" (B5-0677), the AI should consume it at the position-evaluation layer, not invent its own interpretation.
