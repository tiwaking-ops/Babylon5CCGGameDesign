# B5-0337 — Fleet-leadership relation (audit D5)

**Agent:** Buffy (deepseek-v4-flash) · **javac:** 1.8.0_292 · **Scope:**
`b5ccg/src/b5ccg/model/`, `b5ccg/src/b5ccg/engine/` · **Claim:** live-held,
released at close.

## What landed

Implements the rulebook fleet-leadership rule (Action Details, Support or
Oppose: "Characters do not have the Military ability… one character per fleet
may rotate to add his Leadership Ability to the Military Ability of any fleet")
and closes audit deviation D5 from the B5-0203 model audit.

* **`model/FleetCard`** — `leader` relation (null = unled) + accessors;
  `getEffectiveMilitary()`: a rotated, face-up leader adds his Leadership
  (his rotation IS the rulebook requirement for leading; a damaged leader
  adds 0; a rotated fleet contributes 0 regardless); `getPrimaryStatValue`
  for MILITARY now returns the effective value so committed-led fleets and
  any strength readout carry the leadership bonus.
* **`model/Player.conflictTotal`** — the named D5 exclusion: MILITARY totals
  are fleet-based only. Previously every IC character's Leadership fed the
  total directly (double-counting against fleet values); now character
  Leadership enters solely through the relation. Non-MILITARY totals are
  byte-identical to before (ambassador + unrotated IC characters).
* **`engine/RulesEngine`** — `canLeadFleet(p, ch, fl)`: ch is p's ready
  (unrotated, face-up) IC or supporting-role character, fl is p's unrotated
  fleet, and fl has no leader yet ("one character per fleet");
  `executeLeadFleet` rotates ch and seats the relation (no zone change, no
  influence spend); `startRound` clears all leaders — the relation is a
  per-round rotation that expires at the round boundary, exactly like the
  rotations it rides on.
* **`engine/HeadlessConformanceTest`** — new FLR section (15 checks): the D5
  exclusion, non-MILITARY invariance, relation lifecycle, one-leader-per-fleet,
  rotated-leader-cannot-lead-elsewhere, damaged-leader and committed-fleet
  interactions, IC-source leading, round-boundary expiry, and cross-player
  rejection.

## Design boundary

No GameAction type, no AI offer, no UI: the player-facing lead-a-fleet ACTION
is the `lead-fleet` slice of B5-0345 (F3-remainder triage), and ai/ is outside
this row's scope. The engine primitives are public API, ready to be consumed
by that slice — the same seam-first pattern as B5-0323's canRecruit. The AI
keeps playing fully legal turns (smoke: 8 actions, 4/4 legal) because it never
relies on the removed double-count.
