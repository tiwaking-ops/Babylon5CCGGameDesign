## Lessons
- Reactive effect conformance requires the defender-owned enhancement to be on the target owner's list (not the attacker's), and the target fleet must be committed to the conflict before `executeAttackConflictParticipant` — matching the B5-0370 attack-participant test pattern exactly.
- Round-trip PLAY_CARD + attack tests need a fresh GameState/Conflict/RulesEngine after the play, since the play mutates state; reusing the pre-play objects causes stale-state false failures.
