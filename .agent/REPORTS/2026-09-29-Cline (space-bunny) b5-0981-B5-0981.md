| | Lines | `WARNING` lines |
|---|---|---|

The 20 warnings were two distinct defects, each re-emitted once per iteration, so 10
iterations produced 20 lines:

* `[B5-0653] task B5-0481 ...` -- midnight placeholder. **Its ledger row is `DONE`**
  since 2026-09-26.
* `[B5-0952] task B5-0953 ...` -- dated ~640 minutes ahead. **Its ledger row is
  `OPEN`.**

The row's own premise, confirmed: B5-0481 would never be offered anyway, so its ten
lines cannot be preventing anything. They are noise.

## 2. The change, and what it deliberately does not do

Additive, in `.agent/run-queue.ps1`:

1. `$LedgerTerminalStatuses = @('DONE','BLOCKED','SUPERSEDED','VOID')`.
   `OPEN` and `CLAIMED` are **absent on purpose** -- they are the only two statuses the
   offer path can actually withhold work on.
2. `Test-TerminalLedgerRow` -- reads the task's row through the existing
   `Get-LedgerRows`. **UNKNOWN is not terminal**: no readable row returns `$false`, so
   the warning stays loud. This is the B5-0597 failure-3 rule (an absent signal is
   never a benign value) applied to a row lookup rather than to an age.
