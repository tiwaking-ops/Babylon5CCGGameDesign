## Cross-card findings for the data/model tasks (flagged, not ruled)

1. **The "ready" state is printed and unmodelled.** Na'Far, Na'Kal, Shal Mayan all rotate *to
   ready* something. `Card.java` has `faceDown`/`neutralized`/damage state but no `ready`.
2. **Ambassador's Assistant is a whole faction-wide role**, printed on Na'Toth (Narn),
   Ivanova (Human), Vir Cotto (Centauri) — plus Na'Toth's explicit "may replace Ko'Dath".
   No pool field, no model role.
3. **Aftermath redirection** (Neroon) and **tension alteration as an action** (Rathenn) are
   both target/tension verbs outside the pool schema.
4. **Discard-pile-as-resource** (Soul Hunter, both printings) is the first printed mechanic in
   this family that treats the discard pile as a browsable zone with a return-to-play contract.
5. **Leadership/faction counting is printed repeatedly**: Zack Allan counts Human Inner Circle
   characters, Keffer and Shakiri key off "leading" a named fleet. The pool models a
   `leadership` *stat*; none of these read a stat.
6. **Shadow Marks and unrest are printed on non-faction cards** (Urza Jaddo = Centauri,
   Sarah = Human), so neither mechanic is faction-locked in the printed game.
7. **Orb == pool cost now 86 of 86** across B5-0927 + B5-0931 + B5-0933 (11 + 50 + 25), zero
   exceptions in any batch. Zack Allan's missing `cost` is the one open backfill (value 6).
8. **One printed card, two authored effects**: Soul Hunter and Rogue Soul Hunter faces share
   identical rules text while the pool gives them different `text` and different ids — the pool
   invents a distinction the print does not make.
9. **Pool-internal spelling split**: `char_zack_allen` has title "Zack Allan" and text
   "Zack Allen". The face prints "Zack Allan". Recorded as data hygiene, not a rules finding.
