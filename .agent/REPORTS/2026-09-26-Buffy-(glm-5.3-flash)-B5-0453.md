# B5-0453 — AI station-hook awareness (close-out report)

## Status

DONE.

## What was done

Extended the B5-0437 station-influence hooks to the AI scoring layer:

1. **`stationContextScore(GameState)`** method (already present from the twin
   session's initial work) returns an additive score term based on the station's
   current ratings:
   - 0 — all ratings below 15 (today's always-case: pool starts inert).
   - +2 — station influence ≥ 15 (pushes toward the condition-2 threshold of 20).
   - -1 — Shadow War active (shadow or vorlon ≥ 20; station victory is
     suppressed during Shadow War).

2. **MEDIUM scoring** (`scoreActionMedium`): added `stationContextScore` to the
   DECLARE_WAR_CONFLICT case for location-target wars (`a.getTargetCard()
   instanceof LocationCard`). This makes MEDIUM and HARD share the same
   station-context term so both tiers see the rating effects.

3. **HARD scoring** (`scoreActionHard`): already had the term (from twin
   session's initial work); preserved unchanged.

4. **EASY**: unchanged — remains uniform (random pick, no station consultation).

5. **Conformance section**: added `testAIStationAwareness()` in
   `HeadlessConformanceTest.java` with 7 checks verifying:
   - `stationContextScore` = 0 at idle ratings.
   - `stationContextScore` = +2 at influence ≥ 15.
   - Shadow War detection at ≥ 20 shadow influence.
   - `stationContextScore` = -1 when Shadow War active.
   - EASY returns a legal action regardless of station state.
   - MEDIUM returns a legal action at high station influence.

## Reusable lesson

When a station-state scoring method already exists (from prior work) but is
only wired in one difficulty tier, adding it to the other tier is a small,
low-risk change — the key is writing the conformance section to verify the
method's boundary behavior directly (0/+2/-1) plus the EASY-invariant (stays
uniform). This catches any regression in the scoring logic without needing
full game simulations. See also [[b5-0445-docs-suite-count-sync]].
