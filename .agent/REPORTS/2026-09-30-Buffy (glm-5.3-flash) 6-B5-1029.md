section sign should be". Measured:

* `docs/proposals/claim-liveness-protocol-proposal.md` line 4: same,
  `\xc2\xa7`.
* `git show HEAD:` on both files returns the identical `\xc2\xa7` — the
  section sign was never corrupted in any committed state either.
