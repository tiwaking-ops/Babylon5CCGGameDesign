# B5-0354 — Station card research (report-only)

Task: gather every implementable fact about the Babylon 5 station from the
canonical rulebook and the current card data, so B5-0340 (station entity +
checkVictory condition-2 path) can be claimed and implemented without a
research pass. No JSON, source, or governance files were edited (report +
governance close-out only).

## 1. Rulebook ground truth (BABYLON5_CCG_RULEBOOK.md, canonical)

* **:153 (Influence)** — *"In addition to the players, there are other forces
  in the galaxy that may accumulate influence: the galaxy's two most pivotal
  elder races (the Shadows and the Vorlons) as well as the Babylon 5 station
  itself. The Influence Ratings of these non-player forces represent how much
  impact they currently have on galactic affairs. As these Influence Ratings
  climb, certain cards become more potent. As Babylon 5's influence grows, the
  galaxy becomes a safer place, some players will find a wider range of actions
  available, and wars become less common."*
  → The station is a **non-player influence force**, structurally parallel to
  Shadow/Vorlon influence; its rating is a game-state scalar, not a card.
* **:176 (Victory condition 2)** — *"If Babylon 5 has an Influence Rating of 20
  or more at the end of a turn — and one player eligible to win a standard
  victory is leading in Power — then that player wins."*
  → Exact implementation target. Key term: **"end of a turn"** (B5-0340 must
  pick the engine moment — recommendation below).
* **:178 (Shadow War)** — *"If either the Shadows or the Vorlons reach an
  Influence Rating of 20 or more, then the Shadow War begins. During the Shadow
  War, no player may achieve a Standard Victory."*
  → Shadow/Vorlon 20+ **disables Standard Victory entirely** — which includes
  the station-based condition-2 path (it is a Standard Victory). This is a
  **hard prerequisite interaction** B5-0340 must respect, or it would crown
  winners during a Shadow War against the rulebook.
* **:470 (Great War Status Tracker)** — the Great War expansion ships a
  **"Non-Player Forces"** tracker side for *"the relative Influence Ratings of
  the Babylon 5 Station as well as the Elder races of Shadow and Vorlon"* —
  the station is tracked as a first-class non-player force there. No numbers
  are given anywhere in the rulebook.
* **:604 (Aftermaths)** — *"Aftermaths can only be played on the initiating
  faction unless the card specifies Participant"* — unrelated to the station;
  recorded only to delimit this report (no aftermath semantics here).

Data-side findings for B5-0340:

1. **No card in the 446-title pool moves station influence.** No card text
   says "Babylon 5 gains/loses influence", and none references a station
   influence value. The tier-2 "station cards push/pull influence" mechanism
   has **zero data to wire** today.
2. The three `ENHANCEMENT_BABYLON5` cards are the only station-arena effects
   (cost increase / defender bonus); they are owned by whoever plays them
   (owner model unchanged), so the station entity needs no card-ownership
   concept for them.
3. **No card names a station start value.** The dataset cannot supply one.

## 3. Start-value evidence — the decisive finding

* The rulebook **never states a station start value**. The Great War
  tracker (:470) tracks the value but prints no starting number.
* The dataset **cannot supply one** (§2, no station-influence text exists).
* The B5-0332 proposal's Option A explicitly flags "the exact start value
  needs a data check against station-related cards" — **this is that check,
  and it comes back empty**.
* Rules-text corroboration (rulebook :152-155, Influence): player influence
  starts at 4, Build Influence adds +1 for 3 applied; there is no station
  equivalent anywhere. Non-player forces are described only qualitatively.

**Conclusion: the station start value is a design decision, not a data
extraction.** Recommended default for B5-0340: **start 0** with no scheduled
drift — the most conservative rulebook-consistent choice (the rulebook gives
no start value and no drift schedule; 0 keeps condition 2 inert until a
future station-influence source exists, exactly matching today's playtests
where nothing moves station influence). A modest non-zero start (e.g. 5, the
rough midpoint of player start influence 4 and the 20 threshold) is the
alternative if playtesting wants condition 2 reachable in principle; it
would make the *rule* live while remaining data-consistent. Either way the
value belongs in a DECISIONS entry at implementation time, with the constant
named in code (`STATION_START_INFLUENCE`) so the choice is greppable.

## 4. Concrete design inputs for B5-0340 (all verified against code today)

* **Entity**: `Babylon5Station` (model/) with `influence` (int) +
  `getInfluence()/gainInfluence()/loseInfluence()` mirroring `Player`'s
  influence API; a singleton on `GameState` (the rulebook has exactly one
  station). Shadow/Vorlon influence can start as two more ints on GameState
  or a parallel `NonPlayerForce` holder — out of B5-0340's minimal scope, but
  the entity should not preclude it.
* **Victory path**: `RulesEngine.checkVictory` gains condition 2: at the
  **end of round** (the engine's "end of turn" — `GameController.runGame`'s
  round boundary, the same moment `checkVictory` already runs), if
  `station.getInfluence() >= 20` AND exactly one non-forfeited,
  standard-eligible player strictly leads in Power (= influence today, the
  same base the existing `standardVictory()` helper uses) → that player wins.
  The existing D12 `standardVictory()` helper is directly reusable for the
  eligibility/lead predicate (20+ and strictly greatest among non-forfeited
  players — note condition 2 uses the same 20-Power bar for "eligible").
* **Mandatory guard**: condition 2 must be **inert during a Shadow War**
  (rulebook :178 — no Standard Victory during Shadow War). Today nothing
  starts a Shadow War, so the guard is a single `if` on a value that never
  fires — but it belongs in the code from day one, or B5-0340 ships a
  rulebook violation waiting to happen.
* **Traps to avoid**:
  - Do not add station influence to any player's Power total (Power =
    influence + card bonuses; the station is not a player).
  - Do not let condition 2 fire on ties: "leading in Power" + "one player
    eligible" ⇒ strictly-greatest, single-winner predicate — reuse D12's
    strictly-greatest discipline.
  - Do not wire the two "Support Babylon 5" events to station influence —
    their printed effects (gain 1, rotate an enhancement) are **already
    implemented** in CardEffects as player-facing effects and name no
    station value. Wiring them to the station would be fabrication, not
    research-driven implementation.
  - Keep "end of a turn" = end of round (the engine has no finer turn
    granularity); record the interpretation in DECISIONS at implementation.
* **Conformance hooks**: new section (e.g. `STA ×N`): (1) station starts at
  the decided value; (2) below 20, condition 2 never fires even with a
  strict leader; (3) at ≥ 20 + strict single leader → that leader wins; (4)
  at ≥ 20 + tie → no winner (strictly-greatest discipline); (5) major-agenda
  holder is standard-ineligible and cannot be crowned by condition 2 (reuse
  the B5-0305/D12 major-agenda block); (6) Shadow-War-active flag makes
  condition 2 inert; (7) engine without the entity behaves exactly as today
  (no-regression guard).
* **Sequencing**: B5-0340 is the serialize-lane row after B5-0339 (assistant
  mechanic, live-claimed at research time). It has no other unmet
  prerequisite — this report is its data/design input.

## 5. What B5-0340 does NOT get from this report

  against it, not build it).
