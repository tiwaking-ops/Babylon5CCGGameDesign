## Reusable lesson

An "unattributed X" premise must be re-measured against the *current* tree
before it is executed: attribution can arrive between the seed's measurement
and the claim (here, via a bulk restore), and acting on a stale premise would
have stamped `author_llm: unknown` onto five correctly-attributed files —
falsifying provenance in the act of enforcing the provenance rule.
