## Two test-order lessons caught during verification

1. `canPlayCard` gates on hand membership — the legality check must come
   AFTER the hand add, or it fails vacuously (self-caught, fixed in-session).
2. The B5-0470 finding is general: card classes expose setters without
   getters; probe assertions must use the observable state (list membership,
   registry probes, effective-stat read paths), not presumed accessors.

## Reusable lesson

ownership, phase — and a probe must satisfy all of them before asserting
