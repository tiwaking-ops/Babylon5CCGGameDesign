## Data-shape note for the 0473 wiring (per row text)

* NO new JSON keys are needed for the exact-class pair: Censure already
  carries `militaryBonus: −2`, and the B5-0468 seam stores the target at
  play time (`setOpponentTarget(targetCardId, targetOwnerName)`), i.e. the
  target lives in runtime state, not in the card JSON. This matches the
  B5-0420 hidden-agenda precedent (runtime state, zero JSON keys).
* The DELUXE text of de_enh_overworked adds a stat component ("loses 1 from
  all stats") on top of the positional effect — if that card is ever wired,
  its stat half could ride the same explicit-target registry path; its
  positional half cannot. Recorded for the future; not a 0473 concern.
* One interpretive gap for a future wiring slice, flagged NOT fixed: the
  printed text's "(minimum 1)" floor is NOT currently expressible in the
  B5-0357 bonus layer (final clamp is 0 per the D10 floor-at-1 death,
  B5-0367). Censure attached to a Military-1 fleet would take it to 0
  in-engine where the printed rule floors at 1. Options for a future task:
  a per-bonus floor field, or an engine-side special case at the read path.
* Who may SET the target at play time (human UI vs AI auto-choice) is a
  controller-consumer question; the engine wiring (0473) accepts whatever
  target the caller records. Card data remains untouched either way.
