# B5-0966 — provenance and authority hygiene of the DECISIONS.md expansion

Report-only. `docs/DECISIONS.md` was read, never written by this audit except for
the single append-only close-out entry required by `.agent/AGENT_LOOP.md` step 7.
No ledger row other than B5-0966's own was touched, no `b5ccg/src`, no card data,
no commit, no push.

## Headline

**The row's premise is wrong in its load-bearing number, and the reason it is
wrong is itself the finding.** The expansion the row set out to audit is
predominantly a **triple-applied UTF-8-as-cp1252 mojibake regression** across
pre-existing text. Roughly a quarter of the apparent "added lines" are re-encoded
copies of lines that were already in the file. After decoding, the real
expansion is **+2,968 lines, not +4,066**, and the apparent 1,102-line deletion
is **1,098 lines that never went anywhere** plus **4 provenance lines that
legitimately changed**.

### Method for the decoded diff

Each line was pushed back through `cp1252 -> utf-8` repeatedly until a fixpoint
(3 rounds), then `difflib.SequenceMatcher` was run against `HEAD:docs/DECISIONS.md`
(5,037 lines). Zero lines still carry mojibake marks afterwards, so the repair is
complete and not partial. Attribution of the 1,102 raw-deletions was done by
set membership: **1,098 of them appear verbatim in the repaired working tree**, so
they are pure re-encodings; the 4 that do not are:

```
-    - {name: "Buffy (glm-5.3-flash)", version: "glm-5.3-flash"}   (x2, both removed)
-  last_modified_by_llm: {name: "me-so-poor", version: "unknown"}
-  last_modified_date: "2026-09-27"
```

Those four are correct and expected: they are the frontmatter bookkeeping of a
later assessor pass, superseded by the `passes: 4` compacted entry at line 34 and
by `last_modified_by_llm: {name: "Cline (space-bunny)", version: "space-bunny"}`.

## Finding 1 — CRITICAL: `docs/DECISIONS.md` has been triple-mojibake-encoded

* **What:** 1,081 lines of pre-existing decision text are stored as UTF-8 bytes
  that were decoded as cp1252 and re-encoded as UTF-8, three times over. Every
  em-dash became `â†’`, every `§` became `Â§`, every curly quote became
  `â€œ`/`â€`.
* **Evidence:** `## 2026-09-21 — Muse Spark` at working-tree line 67 reads
  `## 2026-09-21 Ã¢â‚¬â€ Muse Spark` and its HEAD counterpart reads the clean
  form. Full examples in the analysis scratch: `Converted lambdaâ†’anonymous
  inner class` where HEAD reads `Converted lambda→anonymous inner class`.
* **Why it matters:** this is a **provenance-integrity** defect, not a cosmetic
  one. AGENTS.md section 1 makes `docs/DECISIONS.md` the record of every
  promotion, every human ruling and every correction. Corrupted bytes inside that
  record mean the file no longer quotes itself, its own prior entries, or the
  proposals it adopts. Any agent grepping for rulebook section references, `§`, or
  any arrow-bearing text will silently miss roughly one entry in seven. The
  B5-0839 lesson — that a detector needs positive *and* negative controls before
  its count means anything — applies directly: `1,081 mojibake lines` is only a
  finding because the three-round fixpoint drives it to **zero**.
* **Contamination of the audit itself:** the naive line count that motivated this
  row overstates the growth by 37%. Any future "DECISIONS.md is bloating, it is
  the largest single-file expansion in the tree" claim is measuring encoding
  damage plus real growth and will keep overstating until the encoding is
  repaired.

**Not fixed here.** The row's scope is explicitly `docs/DECISIONS.md read-only`,
and a repair pass rewrites 1,081 lines of the governance record — a destructive,
separately-claimable operation that needs its own diff review. Filed as a
recommendation below.

## Finding 2 — the load-bearing authority defect: a WITHDRAWN canonical ruling carries no in-entry marker

* **What:** the B5-0654 entry (working-tree lines **4600–4664**) asserts, at line
  4610, `CANONICAL, effective now — the proposal's six operating rules`, sourced
  from `docs/proposals/b5-0388-authenticity-migration-design-proposal.md`.
* **And it was withdrawn.** Two later human rulings reverse it: the B5-0821 entry
  at line 5943 records `B5-0654 WITHDRAWN by human ruling`, and the entry at line
  **7670** is titled `Human ruling: PRINT WINS (replaces withdrawn B5-0654)`.
* **The defect:** the B5-0654 entry itself — all 65 of its lines — contains
  **zero** occurrences of `WITHDRAW`, `withdraw`, `supersed`, `B5-0821` or
  `PRINT WINS`. The retraction lives **3,070 lines further down the same file**.
* **Why it matters:** AGENTS.md section 1a mandates supersede-never-rewrite for
  the pattern store, and the B5-0654 entry honours that by leaving the original
  text alone. But the repo has **no forward-pointer convention for DECISIONS.md
  entries**, so a reader who arrives at the canonical claim by grep — which is how
  a reader arrives at it — reads a withdrawn human ruling as current. `PRINT WINS`
  is the opposite ruling on the same question. An append-only log with no
  supersession marker is an append-only log where the last writer's claim silently
  loses to the first writer's claim.

## Finding 3 — 333 of 373 entries carry no in-entry provenance token

* AGENTS.md section 1 mandates `author_llm` on **`.md` file creation**. `DECISIONS.md`
  satisfies this at file level: `author_llm: {name: "Muse Spark", version: "muse-spark-1.3-contributor-free"}`
  at line 6, and a 49-entry `assessor_llm` list (25 distinct names; 15 entries
  already in the compacted `passes`/`last_pass` form adopted by B5-0655).
* **But** only 40 of 373 `## ` entry bodies contain any `author_llm` /
  `assessor_llm` / `last_modified_by_llm` token. Attribution for the other 333
  lives only in the heading's parenthetical `(model)` token.
* **Sharpened finding:** 20 headings carry **no parenthetical model token at
  all**, e.g. `## B5-0831 - the try_with_resources repair is real`, and the
  `solar-pro4:free` family writes its id bare (8 headings). Those 20 entries are
  attributable to a *name* but not to a *version*, which is exactly the identity
  pair that R5/R6 in `.agent/HEARTBEATS/README.md` and AGENTS.md 1a treat as
  load-bearing.
* Not a violation of section 1 as written — section 1 governs file creation, not
  entry bodies. Recorded as a gap between the rule and the practice.

## Recommendations (each needs its own claim; none performed here)

1. **Repair the mojibake** in `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Mechanical: 3 rounds of
   `cp1252 -> utf-8` to a fixpoint, verified by the same zero-remaining-marks
   check used here. Requires a claim over `docs/DECISIONS.md` and a human-visible
   diff, because it rewrites 1,081 lines of the governance record. Highest value
   item in this report.
2. **Adopt a forward-supersession marker** for DECISIONS entries — a one-line
   `* SUPERSEDED by <entry line> (<date>)` appended inside the *old* entry. This
   is compatible with supersede-never-rewrite (it adds a line, rewrites nothing)
   and it is the only fix that makes a withdrawn ruling safe to grep. Candidate
   for `docs/proposals/`.
3. **Ask whether section 1 should bind entry bodies**, not only file creation.
   As written, 333 entries have no version-level provenance.

## Reusable lesson

**A byte-level diff of a text file measures encoding damage and content change on
the same axis, so before auditing *what a diff added*, decode what it *re-encoded*
— and prove the decode by driving the marker count to zero, not by one round.**
This row set out to audit thousands of lines of new decisions and found 1,098 of
the 1,102 "deletions" were lines that had never left the file.
