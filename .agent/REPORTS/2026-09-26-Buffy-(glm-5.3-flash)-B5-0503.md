## Remainders, each with a one-line slice proposal

1. **S3/K3 card hooks (event sources/sinks)** — blocked on data, not code:
   when the pool gains station-influence cards (human ruling required per
   the standing directive), add a `STATION_EVENT_INFLUENCE` id-keyed table
   in CardEffects (0395 mercenary precedent) consuming
   `gainInfluence/loseInfluence` + `setStationSourceFired(true)`.
2. **S4/K4/T1 tension-matrix soft modifier** — one seam: read
   `state.getTensionMatrix().getTension(src, dst)` inside the S1 capture
   grant to scale +1→+2 (and mirror a −1 loss-of-face when tension is
   0 toward the loser); conformance hooks in the WAR section.
3. **K1 capture-loss sink** — smallest behavioral gap: in the recapture
   branch (RulesEngine.java:400-406), subtract 1 from the ORIGINAL
   capturer's faction rating when the recapturer differs (requires
   remembering who captured — `capturedBy` is nulled by the recapture
   before the loss can be attributed; store the prior owner first).
4. **Shadow presence-bleed (S2 shadow half)** — blocked on a Shadow
   faction player existing in the enum (B5-0354); lands as a 3-line mirror
   of the Vorlon loop the day the faction arrives.

## Verdict

Every proposal hook that could land without new data or a new faction HAS
landed: capture source (S1), Vorlon presence-bleed (S2), decay sink (K2),
condition-2 threshold checks (C2, rulebook-threshold convention), Shadow-War
derived state (W1), and the 0354 trap is honored. The remainders are
exactly the data-gated card hooks (S3/K3), the tension soft-modifier
(S4/K4/T1), and the capture-loss sink (K1) — each mapped to a one-line
slice above, none urgent while ratings stay near zero in real games
(0462/0502 balance runs never move them).

## Reusable lesson

When a proposal ships placeholder thresholds, the implementation that
replaces them with the rulebook's real numbers is a divergence to record
in DECISIONS at implementation time, not a defect to flag at audit time —
audits should check that the divergence was recorded, not that the
placeholders were honored.
