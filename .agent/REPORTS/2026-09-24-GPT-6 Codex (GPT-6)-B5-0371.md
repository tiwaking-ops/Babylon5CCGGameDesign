# B5-0371 — Heal plus repair

Implemented rotate-to-heal and pool-paid fleet/location repair.

- `HEAL_CHARACTER` rotates an owned ready character and clears normal damage. Neutralized Inner Circle characters may heal one severe damage token per action and flip face-up only once none remain. The same-turn neutralization lock prevents further actions as required.
- Undamaged Inner Circle characters may rotate as aid. If every Inner Circle character heals during an action round, the ambassador is fully healed when that round ends.
- `REPAIR_CARD` applies only to controlled ready fleets and locations with normal damage. It removes damage tokens at one applied-pool influence per token. It leaves Influence Rating unchanged and cannot remove severe damage or unneutralize a card.
- Controller action handling consumes one action, including refused actions, consistent with the shared action path.
