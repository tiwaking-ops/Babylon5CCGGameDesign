## The question this row answers

B5-0801 established that 13 of 829 cards carry "power" in title or text, all resolving to Influence, Leadership or card-draw. B5-0809 reads the live engine and reports, card by card, which engine path currently touches each one — CardEffects dispatch tables, RulesEngine legality/victory paths, AgendaCard.isConditionMet, and the INFLUENCE_20 fallback — so the human can decide permanent-or-per-card from measured treatment rather than from a number relayed on authority.

## Cross-cutting observations

1. **Of the 13 cards, exactly zero route through StatKey.POWER.** The word "power" is in a title on all 13 and in the text of some, but every engine path that touches these cards is an Influence path, a Leadership path, a draw path, or a generic fallback that ignores the text. Player.getPower (B5-0677) is read by RulesEngine.canAffectTarget (the Negative Power gate, rulebook :1034) and by the victory-path query for display; it is NOT read by any of these 13 cards' paths.

2. **The Negative Power target gate (canAffectTarget, line 922) is the only engine site that distinguishes "power" from "influence" as a rule concept today.** It fires when a target's Power < its Influence, i.e. when a POWER-tagged bonus has pushed Power below Influence. With no POWER-tagged bonus in play, Power == Influence and the gate is a no-op. The B5-0677/B5-0667 seam therefore already implements the "power can be directly granted without being influence" surface the human named — as a COMPUTED value that can diverge from Influence when a POWER bonus exists — but no card in the current 829 pool exercises it, because none of the 13 power-titled cards (nor any other card) grants a POWER-tagged bonus.

3. **Two cards' texts are silently unimplemented today:** The Price of Power's most-influence loss, and Power Posturing's "Military conflicts gain +1" ongoing. These are not Power-vs-Influence questions; they are generic text-not-implemented gaps that happen to be on power-titled cards. They are inherited from the paraphrase layer and are out of this row's scope; recorded here only because the human's ruling may want to know that "power" on these cards is title-only AND some of the non-power text is also absent.

4. **The printed text the human quoted for A Rising Power** ("Count each 10 Diplomacy you have from ready characters you control as +1 power") does not exist in the repo; the repo's card is an Influence agenda with a Diplomacy-conflict ongoing. If the human supplies that printed text, the engine would need: a per-character Diplomacy source, a per-10 threshold counter, and a Power-tagged (not Influence-tagged) addition to the Power total — which is precisely the "power directly granted without being influence" case, and the canAffectTarget gate would then become exercisable against it.

## The precise permanent-or-per-card question

The human's ruling asks whether Power ≡ Influence is a permanent rule of the engine (every "power" on a card means "influence", forever, and the canAffectTarget guard is enough) or a per-card decision (each power-referencing card must state which it means, and the engine asserts the distinction so a future card cannot silently implement "power" as "influence").

Measured against this tree:

- **Permanent reading today:** the 13 cards are all Influence/Leadership/draw in the engine; the only Power-vs-Influence rule site (canAffectTarget) is already landed and green (10 PWR checks, 643/643 conformance, RUN_TESTS=1 green per B5-0821's measurement). If the human rules "power always means influence in this pool and the guard is sufficient", the engine already conforms to that ruling for all 13 cards, because none of them' paths read Power as anything other than what Influence already supplies.
- **Per-card reading:** the ruling would require that any future or supplied card whose text says "power" be mechanically one of (a) influence (current behaviour for these 13), (b) a POWER-tagged bonus that can diverge from influence (the B5-0677 seam already supports this but no card uses it), or (c) something else (e.g. a threshold counter like the printed A Rising Power text) that the engine does not yet model. Under a per-card ruling, the engine would need an assert-the-equivalence seam: a point where a card whose text says "power" is checked against what the engine actually does with it, so a card that says "power" but is implemented as pure influence cannot silently slip through when a POWER bonus later exists.

## What an assert-the-equivalence seam would need to observe

If the human wants the engine to assert rather than assume the equivalence, the seam needs to observe, at the point a card whose text references "power" is applied:

1. **Card text references to "power"** — today this means a scan of card text for the word "power" (case-insensitive), which B5-0801 already ran and found 13 hits; the seam would need to re-run that scan whenever a card is loaded or edited, not trust the title.
2. **The engine's actual mechanical treatment of that reference** — for each hit, which path touches it (CardEffects table lookup, AgendaCard.isConditionMet, conflict influenceReward, applySimpleAftermathEffect, or generic fallback). Today all 13 resolve to Influence/Leadership/draw or generic floor.
3. **Whether the treatment is "influence" or "a POWER-tagged bonus or threshold"** — i.e. whether the path touches StatKey.POWER or Player.getPower, versus touching getInfluence() or a non-POWER stat. Today all 13 are non-POWER.
4. **A mismatch flag** — raised when a card whose text says "power" is implemented entirely through getInfluence() while a POWER-tagged bonus exists in play, because that is the moment the equivalence can silently break (the canAffectTarget guard protects targeting, not the equivalence itself; it does not catch a card that grants "power" by adding influence and calls it power).

Today, point 4 never fires because no POWER-tagged bonus exists in the 829-card pool (B5-0677 added the channel; B5-0667 proposed it; neither added a card that uses StatKey.POWER as a source). The seam would be a forward-looking guard, not a correction of current behaviour.

## Reusable lesson

**A briefing that answers a ruling question must name the engine path per card, not just the card list.** The B5-0801 list of 13 power-titled-or-texted cards was necessary but not sufficient for the human's ruling — the ruling needs to know that 0 of those 13 paths read StatKey.POWER, that the only Power-vs-Influence rule site (canAffectTarget) is already landed and green, and that two of the 13 cards also carry unimplemented non-power text. A per-card treatment table is the minimum artefact that lets a human rule permanent-or-per-card from measurement instead of from a summary.
