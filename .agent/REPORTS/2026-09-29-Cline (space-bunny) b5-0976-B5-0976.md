# B5-0976 — the B5-0953 claim cannot be offered and cannot be reaped

The row's escalation deliverable is delivered. `.agent/CLAIMS/B5-0953.json` is a
future-dated claim whose negative age makes it un-offerable *and* un-reapable,
and clearing it requires a human. **I recommend the owner re-claim with a real
sampled timestamp, and I recommend against administrative release for now.**

## Why no agent can clear it

1. **The age is negative, so the TTL cannot bite.** `age = now - started_utc` is
   negative; negative compares younger than any TTL. The lock persists for ~11
   hours no matter what else is true. Editing the claim would fix it, but the
   claim belongs to `solar-pro4` and the row forbids it.
2. **The signal set is consistent, so no cross-check catches it.** The claim and
   the owner's heartbeat carry the *same* future instant. A consistency check
   passes; only an external clock disagrees. This is the B5-0597 failure-3
   family reached by a wrong signal instead of an absent one.
3. **A guessing reaper is worse than none.** Three-signal staleness cannot be
   reached while the newest signal is `LIVE`, and
   `.agent/CLAIMS/README.md` requires all three `STALE` plus recorded evidence.
4. **The claimed work has no target.** `ConflictResolution.java` does not exist;
   `engine/` holds 19 other `.java` files. There is nothing for the refactor to
   act on, which weakens the case for urgent administrative release.

## The self-inflicted part, recorded rather than quietly fixed

I first wrote my own claim with a **hand-guessed** `started_utc` of
`2026-09-28T19:58:12Z` while the clock read `19:57:12Z`. The detector printed my
own claim as `-1.1` minutes — a one-minute copy of the exact defect this row
exists to escalate. I re-sampled and rewrote it to `19:57:20Z`, after which the
tool reads `0.1`. **Lesson:** an agent escalating a timestamp defect must catch
it in its own claim first; the discipline is not transferable if the reporter
guessed the same way.

## Options presented to the human

**Option 1 — owner re-claims with a real timestamp (recommended).** `solar-pro4`
rewrites its *own* claim with a clock-sampled `started_utc`, then re-does or
formally abandons the `ConflictResolution` refactor. Preserves scope discipline;
only the owner knows whether the work is wanted.

**Option 2 — human-authorised administrative release.** A human deletes the
stale claim by hand and `engine/` reopens to ordinary claiming. Faster, but
requires destroying another agent's coordination state, which no agent here may
do on its own authority.

**Recommendation: option 1.** Option 2 is acceptable to me as a human decision,
but not as something an agent should do, and the ~11-hour `engine/` blockage
(already costing B5-0969, which is `BLOCKED` on this claim) is a smaller harm
than two writers in `engine/` or a reaper destroying live work.
