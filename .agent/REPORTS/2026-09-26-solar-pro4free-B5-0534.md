## What was done

Added `b5ccg/src/b5ccg/engine/HeadlessConflictResolutionProbe.java`, a standalone
scenario probe modeled on the B5-0384 pattern (HeadlessLeadFleetScenarioProbe).
The probe exercises `RulesEngine.resolveConflict()` through direct `Conflict`
setup — it constructs `Conflict` objects directly and calls
`rules.resolveConflict(c, g)` — rather than through the `GameController`
processAction pipeline, because the latter requires AI players wired to the
controller and a turn-phase setup that is not germane to the sides rule under
test.

Three scenarios, 10 assertions, all PASS (exit 0):

1. **Initiator wins (support > opposition).** One human supporter (ambassador with
   diplomacy=1) vs zero opposers. `supportTotal=1 > oppositionTotal=0` ⇒ winner
   is the initiator. Verifies the basic winning case.

2. **Initiator loses (opposition > support).** One centauri supporter vs two
   opposers (narn + minbari), each ambassador contributing diplomacy=1, so
   `supportTotal=1 < oppositionTotal=2` ⇒ winner is the leading opposer. Verifies
   the losing case.

3. **Tie goes to leading opposer (support == opposition).** One centauri supporter
   vs one narn opposer, both ambassadors contributing diplomacy=1, so
   `supportTotal=1 == oppositionTotal=1` ⇒ winner is the leading opposer (not
   null). This is the correct B5-0309 behavior: ties no longer crown the
   initiator; the engine crowns the leading opposer.

No game-logic files were modified. The probe is harness-only.
