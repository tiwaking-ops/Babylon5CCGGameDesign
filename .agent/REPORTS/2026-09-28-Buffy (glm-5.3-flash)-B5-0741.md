# B5-0741 — Card-data census (report only; no code or data edits)

**Method.** Both card files parsed as JSON
(`b5ccg/resources/cards/premiere.json` — 446 records;
`b5ccg/resources/cards/deluxe.json` — 383 records) and every record's `text`
field scanned per concept family. Classification vocabulary per the B5-0675
precedent: implementable-now / data-gated / deliberately-not-implemented.
Raw-grep counts were taken only as cross-checks, never as the census itself
(see Reusable lesson): "unrest" hits 6, "surrender" 6, "civil" 0, "tension" 0,
"influence" 600 (overwhelmingly the stat name, not a mechanic) across both
files.

## Family A — unrest / same-race tension / Civil War

**Zero card texts invoke any of these axes** in either file. The only
"unrest"-bearing records are `enh_babylon5_unrest` /
`de_enh_babylon5_unrest` and `aftermath_negotiated_surrender` /
`de_am_negotiated_surrender`, whose ids/titles merely contain the words; their
texts are cost-modifier and aftermath effects respectively (below). Class:
**deliberately-not-implemented at the data level** — the engine carries full
unrest / tension / Civil-War machinery (`model/enums/TensionMatrix.java`,
`model/CivilWarState.java`, AIPlayer unrest-pressure wiring per the B5-0727
close-out) while no pool card exercises any of it. This corroborates the
B5-0669 census recorded in that close-out ("no pool card invokes any Civil War
axis"), re-verified on today's tree.

**Newly seedable remainder: none** in this family — a card-triggered Civil War
would require new data fields, not dispatch entries against existing text.

## Family B — surrender

- `de_event_armistice` (deluxe only): "All players must pass for the remainder
  of the round." **Implementable-now** — per-player pass state
  (`isPassed`/`actionsLeft`) and the event dispatch mechanism exist; the event
  would set every player passed at its play window.
- `aftermath_negotiated_surrender` + `de_am_negotiated_surrender` (both files,
  AFTERMATH_MILITARY_PARTICIPANT, triggerCondition MILITARY_PARTICIPANT — a
  supported value per the B5-0311/B5-0317 trigger-vocabulary audit):
  "The loser may keep one fleet from being rotated. Gain 1 Influence." Deluxe
  variant adds "loser also draws 1 card." **Implementable-now** — the
  aftermath play path is live (`GameController.java:600` offers PLAY_AFTERMATH;
  `RulesEngine.canPlayAftermath` gates eligibility), `unrotate()` is an
  existing primitive (RulesEngine ~1369 uses it at round unwind), `drawCards`
  and influence mutation exist. Note: `CardEffects.java` currently carries
  **zero** AFTERMATH entries (grep count 0) — aftermath effects are entirely
  un-dispatched, so this would be the first.

## Family C — influence-as-power (costs, steals, doubling, prevention, penalties)

- **Cost modifiers (4 records: `enh_babylon5_unrest`,
  `de_enh_babylon5_unrest`, `enh_fixed_in_their_ways`,
  `de_enh_fixed_in_their_ways`): DATA-GATED.** No play-cost machinery exists
  in the engine — no play-cost field is consumed and no cost-modifier hook
  exists (`CardEffects` handles stat deltas, conflict steal/penalty, and
  event/agenda effects; not card costs). Wiring needs a cost model + an
  Ongoing-enhancement hook. The deluxe unrest variant ("also increases
  conflict initiation cost by 1") is gated on the same machinery plus a
  conflict-initiation cost concept.
- **`conf_raid_shipping` + `de_conf_raid_shipping`: IMPLEMENTABLE-NOW AND
  ALREADY DISPATCHED** — `CardEffects.java:122-123` carries both ids in
  `CONFLICT_WINNER_STEAL`. The exemplar that the cost family is missing.
  The deluxe conditional escalation ("steal 2 Influence if winner's Military
  exceeds …") is a small extension of that dispatch site: implementable-now.
- **`event_prolonged_talks` + `de_event_prolonged_talks` ("that conflict's
  Influence reward is doubled"): DATA-GATED** — conflict influence rewards are
  structural, with no per-conflict multiplier hook.
- **Penalty cards:** `event_war_by_popular_decree` +
  `de_event_war_by_popular_decree` ("initiate or join a Military conflict this
  round or lose 1 Influence") — **implementable-now** (round-boundary
  conditional; initiation marker and influence mutation exist).
  `event_moral_quandary` ×2 ("one opponent must choose: discard 1 card or lose
  1 Influence") — **data-gated** (needs an opponent-choice mechanic; auto-
  resolving for AI would be a design decision, not a census finding).
  `de_event_the_opposition_rises` and `de_conf_complete_support` forced
  support/oppose clauses — **data-gated** (no forced-commit machinery; cf. the
  participation-restrictions proposal's mustTakeSide decision).
  `de_enh_luxuries_of_homeworld` ("spend 1 Influence to prevent rotation") and
  `de_enh_vital_interests` ("spend 2 Influence to force support/oppose") —
  **data-gated** (spend-to-activate ongoing hooks do not exist).
- **Gain-influence texts (117 premiere / 106 deluxe records):** the dominant
  family. Mostly winner rewards (conflicts) and agenda/event effects flowing
  through existing paths; itemized per-id coverage against the CardEffects
  dispatch maps was beyond this row's scope and is not asserted either way.
  The per-card dispatch mechanism exists (B5-0307); `raid_shipping` proves
  conflict-side per-card dispatch.

## Newly seedable remainder — one proposed slice (the row's maximum)

**Slice: wire the Negotiated Surrender aftermath effect** (both
`aftermath_negotiated_surrender` and `de_am_negotiated_surrender`): gain 1
Influence on play, loser chooses one fleet to un-rotate ("keep from being
rotated"), deluxe variant also draws 1 card. Rationale: it exercises the live
PLAY_AFTERMATH path, is the first-ever aftermath dispatch entry, has two-set
parity in one edit, and every primitive it needs already exists. Runner-up
(not proposed, recorded for the next census): war_by_popular_decree's
round-boundary penalty.

## Reusable lesson

Census the parsed `text` field, never the raw file or the title: "Babylon 5
Unrest" the card has nothing to do with the unrest mechanic, 600 raw
"influence" hits are mostly a stat name, and JSON escaping defeats naive
greps — the same discipline as B5-0675's "classify against data, not the
rulebook", applied one layer down.
