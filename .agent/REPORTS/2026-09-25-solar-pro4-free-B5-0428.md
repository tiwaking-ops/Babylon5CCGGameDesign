## Content summary

- **Three per-station continuous ratings** (human/shadow/vorlon, 0-100, inert from day one) recorded on the Babylon5Station in GameState (B5-0340), read by the readout tasks (B5-0427/B5-0429).
- **Sources**: station capture (LocationCard.capturedBy, B5-0376 Phase B), sustained presence (presence bleed, capped), event-card hooks (no cards added), tension-matrix pressure (soft, read-only from B5-0376).
- **Sinks**: station loss, end-of-round decay (drift toward neutral when no captured locations tied to station), counter-influence event hooks (no cards added), tension-matrix loss-of-face (soft).
- **End-of-round condition-2 checks** (thresholds are placeholders, not wired to any existing B5-03xx rule): Shadow-War-active (shadow > 60 AND vorlon < 30), Vorlon-War-active (symmetric), human-secured (human > 50 AND both below active thresholds), uncontested (all < 20, default + decay target).
- **Shadow-War trigger wiring**: read-only state derived from ratings at end of round — shadow-active, vorlon-active, cold-war (both > 40 but neither > 60), human-secured. No new game action; readout tasks display the derived state.
- **Tension-matrix interaction**: soft modifier on source events (high-tension faction gets small edge; low-tension faction loses small edge), read-only from B5-0376 tension matrix. Not a tension-matrix edit.
- **B5-0354 trap (no Support Babylon 5 rewiring)**: Support Babylon 5 remains a separate player-side card effect with its existing behavior. Station influence does NOT auto-trigger Support Babylon 5, and Support Babylon 5 does NOT auto-raise human influence. They are parallel read paths. If a future card wants to connect them, that is a card-design decision outside this proposal.

## Recommendations

1. Adopt three-rating model as the station-influence design target.
2. Wire readout tasks first (B5-0427 captured-location + war-state, then B5-0429 station + tension) against inert ratings — they would display "0 / uncontested" until a source moves the ratings, validating the read path without changing game behavior.
3. Add card hooks later — once readout tasks confirm the read path, add one or two station-influence cards (sources/sinks) and wire the condition-2 checks to card-driven changes; GSS-measurable design-intent class sets final thresholds at that point.
4. Keep B5-0354 isolated — document the no-rewire trap in engine/model README.
