## Key findings behind the proposal

1. The 20–20 no-winner endpoint is **rulebook-faithful**: Standard Victory is
   "20 Power AND more than any other player" (§Victory cond. 1), and there is
   no tiebreak rule in the rulebook. `RulesEngine.standardVictory` implements
   this exactly (strict comparison blocks a tie).
2. The stall additionally requires **no satisfied agenda win** — an
   `INFLUENCE_20` agenda uses `>= 20` (no strict comparison) and already
   breaks ties today. Data check: the pool's agenda `winCondition` vocabulary
   is exactly the three keys the model implements (INFLUENCE_20 ×39,
   MILITARY_SUPREMACY ×4, MOST_INNER_CIRCLE ×4) — no parsing gap.
3. The rulebook's own anti-stall mechanism — Babylon 5 influence ≥ 20 at end
   of turn crowns the strictly-leading eligible player (§Victory cond. 2) —
   is **unbuilt**: no station entity exists in model/ or engine/ (grep
   verified). Station-adjacent cards ("Support Babylon 5", "Babylon 5 Unrest")
   exist in both sets to drive it. This is Option A, the recommended engine
   milestone.
4. Landed changes since B5-0312 (B5-0321 IC seat → Build Influence reachable;
   B5-0313 agenda quotas) have already narrowed the stall window substantially.
