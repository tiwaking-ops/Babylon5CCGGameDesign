# B5-0339 — Ambassador's assistant mechanic (rulebook §IV, real F10)

**Agent:** Buffy (deepseek-v4-flash) · **javac:** 1.8.0_292 · **Scope:**
`b5ccg/src/b5ccg/model/`, `b5ccg/src/b5ccg/engine/` · **Claim:** live-held,
released at close. B5-0352 held a live claim on `resources/` throughout —
untouched.

## What landed

Implements §IV "Your Ambassador's Assistant": "You may rotate your assistant
to give your ambassador +1 to Diplomacy, Intrigue and Leadership while the
assistant remains rotated, or to allow your ambassador, later that turn, to
apply 1 influence less than usual when sponsoring a card… your assistant must,
of course, be ready and unneutralized."

* **`model/CharacterCard`** — `assistantBonus` flag + accessors. The flag is
  read by `getPrimaryStatValue` (+1 Diplomacy/Intrigue/Leadership when set and
  the card is face-up; **Psi untouched** — the rulebook forbids raising Psi
  from a base of 0 with generic ability bonuses). The bonus is computed, never
  mutated into base stats, so every consumer (conflictTotal, the conflict
  resolution ambassador path) sees it consistently, and damage un flags nothing.
* **`model/Player`** — `sponsorDiscount` with grant/consume semantics
  (`consumeSponsorDiscount` applies up to n and returns what was used).
* **`engine/RulesEngine`** — `canUseAssistant` (own ready, unneutralized
  supporting-role assistant + own ambassador); `executeAssistantAbilityBoost`
  (rotates the assistant, flags the ambassador — bonus lasts while the
  assistant remains rotated, i.e. expires with the round); 
  `executeAssistantSponsorDiscount` (rotates the assistant, grants 1 discount
  for later that turn); `baseRecruitCost`/`recruitCost` split — effective
  sponsor cost = base (doubled-for-other-race) minus discount, floored at 0;
  `startRound` clears all bonus flags and the discount with the turn.
* **`engine/GameController`** — the recruit site spends the effective cost and
  consumes **exactly** the applied discount (a partial discount on a
  cheaper-than-discount card is consumed correctly), logging the discounted
  sponsor.
* **`engine/HeadlessConformanceTest`** — new AST section (19 checks):
  readiness gating (rotated/damaged/foreign/non-supporting refused), boost
  mechanics (+1 Dip/Leadership via the stat reader, Psi untouched, visible in
  conflictTotal), non-stacking binary flag, discount grant/cost-drop/floor-0/
  canRecruit/exact-consumption/exhaustion, and round-boundary expiry of both
  effects.

## Interpretations recorded (full text in DECISIONS.md)

* **Attachment:** the rulebook says the assistant is "attached to a specific
  ambassador" and §League adds cross-faction ownership ("in another player's
  faction") — the model has no card-ownership graph, so an assistant assists
  his controlling player's own ambassador. The check takes the ambassador as a
  parameter, so the true attachment is a drop-in when ownership exists.
* **Non-stacking:** the flag is binary; a second assistant does not stack
  (+1/+1/+1 is the rulebook grant; stacking is unaddressed and conservative).
* **"Sustained" bonuses:** recorded as unimplemented — sustaining past the
  assistant's unrotation has no precedent in the engine's effect layer.
* The UI readout remains B5-0347 (explicitly out of this row's scope); with
  the mechanic real, the F10 readout is now unblocked and no longer at risk of
  displaying fabricated content.
