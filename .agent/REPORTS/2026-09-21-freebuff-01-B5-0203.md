# B5-0203 — rulebook-conformance audit of `b5ccg/src/b5ccg/model/` (report-only)

Agent: `freebuff-01`. Toolchain: `javac 1.8.0_292` (`javac -version`).
Scope: report-only; **no file under `b5ccg/src/` was modified**. Gate re-verified
green after the audit (`sh b5ccg/compile.sh` → 34 files, `-source 6 -target 6`,
only the expected bootstrap-classpath warning; HeadlessSmokeTest exit 0).

Method: every model file read in full, cross-checked against the canonical
rulebook (`BABYLON5_CCG_RULEBOOK.md`; sections cited by heading) and — where a
finding touches how a model API is consumed — corroborated read-only against
`engine/RulesEngine.java` / `engine/GameController.java` (not edited, not in
scope). Card JSON in `b5ccg/resources/cards/` consulted where it feeds model
fields (e.g. `triggerCondition` values).

## Confirmed conformant (no action)

1. **Character stats & damage model** — Diplomacy/Intrigue/Psi/Leadership;
   `isFaceDown()` zeroes the stat in `getPrimaryStatValue` — matches
   "neutralized contributes nothing"; face-down ≠ discarded matches Draw Round
   Step 1 (neutralized supporting cards discarded, Inner Circle kept — the
   discard is engine-side; model distinguishes correctly).
2. **Conflict types** — exactly Diplomacy/Intrigue/Military/Psi (rulebook
   conflict-type table). Characters use Diplomacy/Intrigue/Psi and map
   Leadership→Military ("characters do not have the Military ability"; the
   Leadership-to-fleet rule is an engine concern — noted below, out of scope).
3. **Fleet rotation gating** — `FleetCard.getMilitary()` returns 0 while
   rotated: consistent with "most cards may only act once per turn" and the
   rotate-to-participate action rule.
4. **Conflict win condition (support > opposition)** — `Conflict.playerTotal`
   + `RulesEngine`'s strict `>` comparisons (support must *exceed* opposition);
   tie ⇒ initiator did not win ⇒ "lost", as required for aftermath eligibility.
5. **Influence floor** — `loseInfluence` floors at 0; consistent with the
   rulebook's token-physical influence economy (rulebook has no negative
   ratings; the Negative Power section concerns card text referencing Power,
   not this).
6. **Draw-round reconstruction** — `Deck` + `Player.drawCards`: one free card
   per player (`RulesEngine.drawPhase`); deck-out ⇒ `recycleDiscard`, then
   nothing. See deviation D8 — the required penalty is missing.
7. **Agenda uniqueness** — `Player.setAgenda` holds a single agenda slot,
   matching "you may have only one agenda in play at a time".
8. **Deck draw/discard structure** — face-up discard next to the draw deck,
   examine-any-time: `Deck.discard`/`getDiscardPile` (unmodifiable view)
   match; `shuffle`/`peekTop` are legitimate extras, not violations.

## Deviations (each with rulebook section; smallest-fix suggestion; NOT applied)

**D1 — Aftermath "Won/Lost" evaluated from the wrong player's perspective.**
`AftermathCard.isEligible(playerWon, participated, conflictType)` is sound, but
`RulesEngine.canPlayAftermath` computes `playerWon = (winner == p)` — the
rulebook (Aftermath Cards, `won`/`lost` play conditions; Aftermath Example
table) defines Won/Lost from the *initiator's* outcome: "Conflicts in which a
non-initiator side gains more support than the initiator's side are considered
to have been 'lost' by the Initiator". A non-initiator participant who *opposed
successfully* should be playing a "Lost"-condition aftermath, but the engine
marks him "won". Smallest fix lives in engine/ (B5-0202's scope): pass
`won = (winner == conflict.getInitiator())`.

**D2 — "Participant" aftermaths ignore the initiative-faction restriction.**
Rulebook: "Aftermath cards may normally be played only upon the faction that
initiated the just resolved conflict… if 'Participant' is one of the Play
Conditions, then appropriate aftermath cards may target cards in play for any
faction that either Supported, Opposed or Attacked" (Aftermath Cards /
Participant). Model has no notion of *target* at all; the engine plays any
eligible aftermath on the *playing* player himself. Report-only: this needs a
target concept on AftermathCard or at the play site.

**D3 — Confusion between Aftermath play conditions and card text conditions.**
JSON `triggerCondition` values are compound: `WON_DIPLOMACY`,
`MILITARY_PARTICIPANT`, `LOST_MILITARY`, `AFTERMATH_ANY` ("ANY"), etc.
`isEligible` substring-tests only WON/LOST/PARTICIPANT/DIPLOMACY/MILITARY/
INTRIGUE. Two holes: (a) `WON_DIPLOMACY` requires both won AND diplomacy —
substring checks AND them, which happens to work, but `LOST_MILITARY` on a
*diplomacy* conflict: `contains("LOST")` ✓, `MILITARY` ⊄ `LOST_MILITARY`... 
actually passes only if the conflict is military — the substring `MILITARY`
check correctly rejects non-military. The real hole: `PSI` conflicts reject
*every* typed aftermath (`contains("MILITARY"|"DIPLOMACY"|"INTRIGUE")` all
false ⇒ eligible), i.e. **aftermaths meant for other types are legal in Psi
conflicts**, and an aftermath with `triggerCondition: "PSI"` (none exists
today) would have no matching branch. Rulebook: Type of Conflict — "aftermath
cards may only be played following the resolution of a conflict of the
appropriate type". Smallest fix: add a PSI branch / exhaustive type match in
`AftermathCard.isEligible`.

**D4 — No "only one of each named aftermath per target" guard.**
Rulebook (Aftermath Cards; Bonuses and Blanking): "only one of each named
aftermath may be in play on the same target". The engine's aftermath play is
discard-after-immediate-effect, so nothing persists — but the model offers no
support for the persistent variant (compare D2). Record-only.

**D5 — `Player.conflictTotal` double-counts Leadership.**
It adds every Inner Circle character's `getPrimaryStatValue(MILITARY)` —
which *is* their Leadership — directly to the military total. Rulebook
(Action Details, Support or Oppose): "Characters do not have the Military
ability… one character per fleet may rotate to add his Leadership Ability to
the Military Ability of any fleet". Leadership should only enter via an
explicit lead-a-fleet action (one leader per fleet, and leading requires
rotating). Smallest fix: exclude characters from MILITARY totals unless a
fleet-leadership relation exists (needs a new model relation + engine wiring;
flag for a future task, not B5-0202's smallest set).

**D6 — `Player.resetActions` grants 1 action; rulebook has unlimited actions
per player per action round.**
Action Round: players act in initiative cycles, "as many times as necessary",
until all pass consecutively. One-action-per-round makes the action round
structurally different (each player gets exactly one action). This is a
deliberate-looking simplification rather than an accident; it also caps
Build-Influence (rotate + apply 3, ≤ rating 9 — itself unimplemented, see D7)
and sponsoring. Fixing means reworking GameController's pass-loop, not the
model constant alone. Record-only.

**D7 — Build Influence action absent from model (and engine).**
Rulebook action list + "Rotate to Build Influence": rating ≤ 9, rotate an
Inner Circle character, apply 3 influence ⇒ rating +1. No model
representation (no action type in `GameAction.Type`, nothing on `Player`).
Related: influence is never *applied/spent-temporarily* as the rulebook
describes (applied influence restored at start of turn); `spendInfluence`
exists but no restore-to-rating cycle exists — `Player.influence` is one
merged number. Record-only; larger design question.

**D8 — Deck-out penalty missing: must discard an Inner Circle character, else
lose the game.**
Draw Round Step 3: "If a player's draw pile is empty when he must draw at
least 1 card, he must instead discard 1 Inner Circle character. If a player
cannot discard an Inner Circle character, he loses the game." `Player.drawCards`
silently reshuffles the discard pile forever (also contrary to "Players who
draw through their entire play deck… may not reshuffle"), and never triggers
the loss. Smallest fix: in `drawCards`, when deck empty → remove a non-ambassador
Inner Circle character ( ambassadors may never be discarded) and signal
forfeit (e.g. log + a state flag the engine's victory check can read).

**D9 — `Player.influence` conflates Rating and available (unapplied) influence.**
Rulebook Influence: Rating starts at 4 (✓ `influence = 4`), applied influence is
restored each turn; Rating changes are permanent gains/losses. Model has a
single integer, so "apply influence for an action" permanently reduces the
Rating in any caller that would use `spendInfluence` for it. (No current
caller does; the concept is simply absent.) Related to D7. Record-only.

**D10 — Minor: `FleetCard.applyMilitaryDelta` floors at 1.**
`military = Math.max(1, military + delta)` — no rulebook text justifies a
floor of 1 (military ratings can be 0 for locations, and reductions exist in
card text). Also, like `CharacterCard.applyStatDelta`, it mutates the printed
value permanently rather than tracking a modifiable bonus layer, which will
conflict with the rulebook's cumulative-effects/blanking rules when effects
gain expiry (cf. EventCard "lasts only until the end of the current turn").
Record-only; harmless until effect durations exist.

**D11 — Minor: `CharacterCard.applyStatDelta` floors every stat at 0.**
Same pattern as D10; additionally violates the rulebook's Psi note in spirit
("Psi cannot be raised from a base of 0 unless a card specifically increases
Psi") — nothing enforces that distinction today (any delta raises Psi). And
`EnhancementCard` bonuses (five getters, incl. `psiBonus`) have **zero
callers** anywhere in `b5ccg/src/` — enhancements are added to
`Player.enhancements` and never applied. That is an engine gap (B5-0202's
scope is the AI; routing the engine-side enhancement application to a new
task) — the model side is conformant as a data holder.

**D12 — `AgendaCard.isConditionMet` ties are wins; rulebook requires *more* than
any other player.**
Victory (standard): "Have 20 Power, and more than any other player".
`MILITARY_SUPREMACY` uses strict `>=`-rejection (`mil >= ownerMil` ⇒ not
supreme) ✓, but `MOST_INNER_CIRCLE` also requires strictly-most ✓ — however
both ignore that the *standard* path needs "more than any other player" and
`INFLUENCE_20` checks only `owner.getInfluence() >= 20`. Since
`RulesEngine.checkVictory` returns the *first* player meeting his condition,
a tie at 20 influence makes the earlier-indexed player win. Also: standard
victory should be blocked for major-agenda holders ("If you have a major
agenda in play, you cannot score a standard victory") — `isMajorAgenda` is
stored but never consulted, and the fallback branch returns
`influence >= 20` for any unknown key. Smallest fix: pass major-agenda state
into the check and require strictly-greatest power for the standard path.

**D13 — `Faction.isPlayableBy` treats NON_ALIGNED as universally playable and
ignores the double-cost rule.**
Rulebook (Character Cards / Sponsor action): loyal characters of another race
cost *double* (not unplayable), restricted (race-named type) cards are
unplayable to other races, neutral characters are cost-neutral for everyone.
`NEUTRAL` and `NON_ALIGNED` both return true for everyone — but per the
rulebook "Non-Aligned IS a race name": NON_ALIGNED cards should be restricted
to a NON_ALIGNED player (League faction), playable-by-others only via the
double-cost rule which the model cannot express (no cost field exists in
model/ or the card JSON at all — `grep '"cost"'` over both sets: 0 matches).
Smallest model-side fix: NON_ALIGNED should behave as a normal race for
`isPlayableBy`; double-cost needs a cost field first. Deck-cost dimension is
out of model scope.

**D14 — Only 4 conflict types exist but data/classes have no PSI stat source
beyond characters — fine; however `Conflict` (state) has no support/oppose
distinction.**
`Conflict.commitCard(Player, Card)` records cards but not *which side* they
supported; `JOIN_CONFLICT_SUPPORT/OPPOSE` exist in `GameAction.Type` yet the
engine only auto-commits ambassadors. Per the conflict-resolution rule, the
initiator wins only if support > opposition — with side-less commitment,
`RulesEngine` fakes this with per-participant totals (highest total wins).
Model-side gap: `Conflict` cannot express opposition. Record-only (fix spans
engine; flag for a future task).

**D15 — Trivia/minor: `ConflictCard.influenceReward` has no rulebook-side
validation** (conflict cards in the real game may instead *remove* influence
or do other effects; e.g. `de_conf_loss_of_support` text says "Loser loses 1
Influence and must discard one card" — the JSON only carries
`influenceReward` for the winner). Model is a plain holder; the text-driven
effects are simply unimplemented anywhere. Consistent with the generic
effect-text gap already noted (CardEffect dead, D11 enhancement gap).
Record-only.

## Cross-cutting observation: dead effect plumbing

`CardEffect` (`apply(GameState, Player, Card)`) has no implementers and no
callers in `b5ccg/src/`. All per-card text effects (events, conflicts'
loser-penalties, enhancements' bonuses, agenda ongoing effects) are
unimplemented; the engine approximates them generically (Event ⇒ draw 1).
This is the root cause behind several items above (D2, D4, D10, D11, D15).
The model layer is largely *conformant as data*; the conformance gaps are
mostly *missing effect application*, which is an engine feature, not a
model edit. Recommend a follow-up task (e.g. "B5-0204: implement
CardEffect dispatch for Enhancement + Agenda effects") before B5-0203's
findings can be fully fixed.
