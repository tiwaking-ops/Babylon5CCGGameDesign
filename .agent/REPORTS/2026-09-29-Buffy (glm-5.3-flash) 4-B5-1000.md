## Result

Ledger went from **12** non-7 rows to **exactly 10** — only the two adjudicated
repairs moved. No normalise-to-seven was performed; no status, claim column,
task text, or verified text changed; no row renumbered. Gates:
`run-dup-census.ps1` PASS exit 0; detector confirms the 10 remaining rows are

## Reusable lesson

**A pipe-repair wave is a classification verdict, not a subtraction.** Nine of
the twelve "defects" are content pipes the ledger's own history has already
adjudicated as protected; only one row was additively missing a pipe and one
carried a genuinely structural excess. The deliverable is the per-row evidence
table plus two minimal repairs — and the discipline of leaving the protected
set byte-identical is what keeps the previous adjudications true.
