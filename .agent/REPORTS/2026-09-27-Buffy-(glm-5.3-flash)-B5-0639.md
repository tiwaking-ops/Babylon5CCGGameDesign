## THE one proposed slice

**R5 — Unconditional Surrender (:815), one engine slice.**
Rationale: it is rulebook-contained (surrender during the discard round to a
war opponent, +3 influence to the captor, asylum copy as a clean supporting
character), it needs no new data fields (unlike R2/R7/R14), no cross-cutting
engine split (unlike R15), and no multi-faction structure (unlike R11–R13).
It reuses the existing forfeit/last-standing plumbing asserted by MJR, and
its 3-influence gain + clean-copy semantics are directly assertable through
the existing `setInfluence`/Inner Circle fixtures. Proposed section code:
SUR, 6–8 checks, scope `engine/` + one suite section.

## Reusable lesson

Map a rulebook section subsection-by-subsection and record what is
deliberately NOT implemented (optional rules, meta, exemplary text) alongside
the gaps — a gap list that does not separate "not yet" from "not applicable"
makes every later coverage pass re-derive the same triage from scratch.
