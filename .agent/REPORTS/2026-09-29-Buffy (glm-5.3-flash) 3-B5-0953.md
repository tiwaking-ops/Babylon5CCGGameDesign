Repaired: UNKNOWN on a missing or unjoinable owner signal is answered UNSTEALABLE (`$true`, the same disposition as the B5-0653/B5-0952 refusals) and the task is NOT offered, with a loud warning naming id and owner. A claim with an unreadable `agent_id` gets the same disposition — an unjoinable owner signal is the same epistemic state — matching the pre-existing UNPARSEABLE-claim branch ("UNKNOWN is never LIVE") that already refused exactly this shape. Direction rule preserved from B5-0653: wrongly withholding costs one blocked task the owner clears in seconds; wrongly offering costs a collision and destroyed work. The stale ghost this can leave on a dead owner is a reap question under 00_BOOT step 10 (all three real signals STALE, evidence recorded in the reap note first), never an offer question.
2. **Suppression path** (`Get-CensusSuppression`): fell through to claim/report age and read `reportable` where ledger-query suppressed. Repaired by converging the copy to ledger-query's semantics with identical marker strings (`<unreadable agent_id>`, suppress on `no-heartbeat`), so `census-crosscheck.ps1` proves the copies agree rather than adjudicating between them.

`census-crosscheck.ps1` replicas updated in lockstep: `Get-Verdict-R` now answers `unknown:no-heartbeat` / `unknown:agent_id-unreadable` / `unknown:unreadable-claim`, `Get-Suppression-R` mirrors `Get-Suppression-L` exactly, and the header history records the B5-0649 divergence as retired rather than deliberate.
