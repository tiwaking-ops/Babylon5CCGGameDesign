## Reusable lesson

Seam tests should probe three things in one method: (1) the default/legacy
state, (2) the explicit-target transition, and (3) a registry probe round-trip
(`grantBonus` → `hasAttachedBonusFrom`). Keeping all three concerns local to the
seam under test makes coverage review fast and regression isolation trivial.
