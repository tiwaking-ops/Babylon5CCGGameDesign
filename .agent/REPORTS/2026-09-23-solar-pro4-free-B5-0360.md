# B5-0360 — Economy modeling proposal (report)

## Deliverable

Proposal doc: `docs/proposals/b5-0360-economy-modeling-proposal.md` — covers
three spend/wire topics (double-cost pool interaction, mercenary bid model,
free-participant waiver concept) with B5-0342 (D9 pool) as load-bearing
dependency.

## What was produced

Three separable proposal slices, all assuming B5-0342's influence pool is live:

**E1 — Double-cost as pool spend (thin).** `baseRecruitCost` already computes
`cardCost * 2` for other-race loyal characters, but it was expressed as a Rating
computation rather than an explicit rule. Proposal: add a named helper
`isDoubleCostRequired(CharacterCard, faction)` so the rulebook :659 double-cost
rule is greppable; neutralize the neutral exemption with an explicit comment; the
spend becomes `applyInfluence(effectiveCost)` against the pool (not Rating
mutation). No new model field, no data change, no behavior change at today's
costs.

**E2 — Mercenary bid model (deep, proposal shape only).** Defines contracts for
a future implementation: `BID_ON_MERCENARY` action type, per-mercenary bid state,
cumulative-bid resolution after the pass phase, a new `MERCENARY` phase between
ACTION end and CONFLICT_RESOLUTION, and the card-data prerequisite (B5-0355
text audit + a data task to flag mercenary cards). Open questions recorded:
bid turn-ordering (simultaneous pooled vs turn-ordered), tie-break, which cards
are mercenaries. Not implemented — contracts only.

**E3 — Free-participant waiver as a first-class concept (medium).** Models the
§Free waiver (:1154) as a `SponsorCost` result type with `amount`, `requiresRotation`,
`isWaived` flags, driven by card-text effects in the existing `CardEffects`
registry (B5-0307 design). Non-Aligned Support's "free participant" (Q4 ruling)
is the canonical example — a join with waiver has amount 0 and no rotation, must
still meet other restrictions. Unifies §Free semantics across sponsor and join
paths. The waiver concept is the deliverable; the wiring to the effect registry
is a future task.

## Dependency (load-bearing)

B5-0342 (D9 pool) is required first. Without it, every sponsor permanently
weakens the Rating (the D9 defect), and implementing E1/E2/E3 before B5-0342
would bake that defect into the economy model. The dependency is recorded in
§4 of the proposal doc with a spend-comparison table.

## Open questions deferred

1. Mercenary tie-break (highest bidder wins; ties → current controller, no one,
   or initiative order — unspecified).
2. Mercenary card identification (no data field; needs B5-0355 + data task).
3. Bid turn-ordering (simultaneous pooled vs turn-ordered — rulebook ambiguous).
4. Waiver scope: does §Free waive the double-cost too? (Rulebook says "no
   influence cost" — should waive the entire sponsor cost including double, but
   not explicit; implementation task records interpretation.)
5. Non-Aligned Support specifically: Q4 ruling applies §Free to joining, not
   sponsoring — the two are parallel under this proposal but wired to different
   action paths.
