concurrent writer (GPT-6 Codex closing B5-0387) inserted a row between my

cells, B5-0390 DONE with close-out, total delta exactly +closeout bytes.

**Lesson (also in DECISIONS):** on the shared ledger, never anchor edits to

"—") that do NOT round-trip through str_replace; byte-exact perl one-liners
scoped to exact row content are the safe tool for those cells.
