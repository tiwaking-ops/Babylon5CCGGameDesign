## AI anomaly observations

1. **Stall at 4–5 rounds:** Every game stalled before a winner. The 60s timeout hit at round 4–5 consistently. This matches the known D6 limitation (B5-0341/B5-0372): the current pass loop terminates after one pass per player per round (actionsLeft cap), so influence-building is slow and games don't converge within the timeout window.

2. **Zero promotions:** No IC promotions occurred despite Build Influence being used 44 times across 5 games. This is consistent with the B5-0321 prerequisite gap: Build Influence is available but IC promotion requires the ambassador to be seated in innerCircle first (seated per B5-0321, but the AI may not be choosing to promote). The Promote action is offered by the AI (MEDIUM/HARD scoring) but may be deprioritized against cheaper actions.

3. **Zero agendas:** No agendas were set in any game. Agenda-setting requires a player to reach 20 influence AND play an agenda card. With Build Influence being the primary action (44 uses) and games stalling at low influence totals, the 20-influence threshold for agenda play may not be reached before the timeout.

4. **Conflict asymmetry:** Initiators won 17 of 24 trackable conflicts (71%), lost 7 (29%). This is consistent with the B5-0309 sides rule (support > opposition wins for initiator) and the AI's tendency to initiate when it believes it can win.

5. **Aftermath usage:** 15 aftermaths played across 5 games (avg 3/game). Aftermaths are the secondary action after conflict initiation, used for payback/retaliation.

6. **No mercenary bids observed:** The mercenary bid UI (B5-0404) and AI bidding (B5-0403) are implemented but no mercenary cards exist in the pool (B5-0386: pool carries zero mercenary evidence). The MERCENARY phase and BID_ON_MERCENARY action type exist in the engine but are never triggered because no mercenary cards are in play.

## Known gaps (with task links)

- **D6 unlimited actions (B5-0341/B5-0372):** The single-pass-per-round limitation is the root cause of the stall. Until D6 lands, multi-round probes will consistently timeout.
- **No mercenary cards (B5-0386):** Mercenary bidding exists in engine/AI/UI but has no data to exercise.
- **Agenda threshold unreachable:** Without D6 or a faster influence economy, agenda victory is not reachable in headless probes.
- **No damage/heal/repair:** Tier-3 actions (B5-0370, B5-0371) are not yet implemented, so conflict resolution is purely diplomatic (no MAP damage).
