# B5-1032 - A home for the annotated deluxe errata

Report-only: no JSON edited, no errata authored, no src touched. Instruments
named: python3 json.load over both card files (title-join per the DeckLoader
dedup key), annotation-stripping diff of every deluxe/premiere text pair,
extraction of the full CardEffects id-keyed dispatch tables (53 ids), and
line-level reads of the dispatch sites. Denominator note: the row says 124
annotated; my title-join measures **150** annotated text divergences (the twin
census's 325-pair denominator vs my all-deluxe-records denominator — same
reconciliation B5-1021 recorded; the four unannotated cards are B5-1021's
territory and excluded here).

## The structural finding that reframes the row

**148 of the 150 annotated deluxe texts are core-identical to their premiere
twin** once the `(Deluxe ...)` parenthetical is stripped. The deluxe *rule
surface* is the premiere text; the erratum lives only in the annotation (or in
structured fields). The two exceptions are the unannotated pair (B5-1021:
prophecy extends, sunhawk truncates). The wave's four named examples —
adira_tyree, bester, emperor_turhan, general_hague — are all **core-identical**:
their annotations *clarify* text that already carries the (premiere=deluxe)
rule, and the structured fields agree with the clarifications where fields
exist. The feared class — "substantive rules change existing only as prose that
contradicts the structured fields" — is **much smaller than the row's premise**:
the delta between what the engine executes and what the deluxe card promises
sits in exactly two places, quantified below.

## The triage (per the row's three categories)

Category rule, stated: WRONG = the engine *executes* a rule for that specific
deluxe card and the executed rule contradicts or misses the deluxe delta; RIGHT
= the engine executes (or already carries in structured fields) the deluxe
delta; PRESENTATIONAL = no rule the engine executes differs (includes all 18
pure provenance annotations — art, redistribution, reprint status — and every
card whose effect family is engine-silent for *both* editions, which is the
known prose-effect gap, not 138 separate WRONGs).

|---|---|---|
| **RIGHT** | 5 | `de_am_negotiated_surrender` (deluxe draw-1 branch implemented at CardEffects.java:441); `de_agenda_servants_of_order` (2-character threshold special-case at :486); `de_enh_latent_telepath` and `de_enh_power_posturing` (structured psiBonus 3 / militaryBonus 2 carry the deluxe value the annotations describe); `de_agenda_higher_calling` (isMajorAgenda true in both editions carries the "now Major" change). |

| **PRESENTATIONAL / engine-silent** | 138 | Annotations on cards whose effects neither edition dispatches (the B5-0741 family finding), plus the 18 provenance annotations. No executed rule can contradict the held card because no rule executes. |

| # | Card | Premiere rule implemented | Deluxe delta NOT implemented |

| 1 | `de_agenda_total_war` | +1 Military fleets (AGENDA_FLEET_PLUS1) | "restriction now also prohibits playing Diplomacy Aftermath cards" (the core's Diplomacy-conflict restriction is itself unenforced, but the *delta* is the Aftermath extension) |

Common shape: **the deluxe delta is an *addition* to a dispatched effect, and
the dispatch tables were built from the core text.** A player holding any of
these seven deluxe cards gets strictly less than the card promises, silently.

Separately named (data-level, not WRONG): 11 annotations assert bonus numbers
the structured fields do **not** reflect (ipsha battleglobe dip +1, warleaders
unconditional +3, shadow_assault +2, concentrated_effort +4, coordinated_fire
min 2, military_cadre/ranger_strike/secret_police/wind_swords +3/+4,
defense_in_depth 3, bester replace-not-add) — these live on engine-silent
effect families and cannot contradict anything the engine executes today, but
they are the first casualties the moment those families are wired.

## Proposed structured representation (one; authoring is human-ruled)

A per-card optional **`deluxeErrata` object**: `{ "kind": "RULES" |
"PRESENTATIONAL", "delta": "<machine-readable one-line description>",
"source": "<printed-card citation>", "implemented": true|false }`. The loader
would then: strip annotations from the displayed rules text (players read the
core), expose `kind=RULES` deltas to the engine with an explicit
implemented/unimplemented status, and let census tools count unimplemented
RULES errata as a gate-able number instead of grepping prose. Critically,
**authoring must wait for the printed cards**: the current parentheticals are
themselves unverified transcriptions, and structuring an unverified claim makes
it load-bearing (the same reason the row forbids authoring here).

## Reusable lesson

An erratum that lives in prose is invisible to the engine, but an erratum that
lives in prose **on a dispatched effect** is a silent nerf — triage the
annotations by whether their host card's effect family executes at all, and the
140-card wall collapses into a 7-card list with one shared root cause
(dispatch tables built from core text).
