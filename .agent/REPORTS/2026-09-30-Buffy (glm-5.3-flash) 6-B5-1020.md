## What shipped in ledger-query.ps1

A `defectReport` classification, between the existing claims-first suppression
(which keeps precedence) and `reportable`:

* **Classes (only these):** excess pipes strictly inside a *double-quoted*
  span, inside a *backtick* span (delimiters paired), or on a row carrying the
  explicit in-row prior-adjudication marker — the literal phrase
  **"left intact"**, which is the convention the three adjudicated rows
  themselves carry (`[prior adjudication: excess pipes left intact]`).
* **Exact-7 arithmetic backstop:** `pipeCount − exemptPipes == 7` must hold
  exactly; anything left over, over-stripped, or unclassifiable stays
  `reportable`. Position-based, not row-based.
* **Never exempt:** doubleLead rows (the lead-pipe repair duty is independent
  of the count), rows with unpaired quote delimiters, single-quote spans
  (apostrophes make them unsafe — B5-0490's `'420s/...'` inside backticks
  proved the trap), and any row whose excess is bare prose (no operator or
  parenthetical exemption exists).
* **Footer receipt added:** `-- B5-1020: N row(s) content-exempt; M further
  row(s) at a non-7 pipeCount remain reportable. A new free pipe in plain
  unquoted prose is still a defect. --` — the standing state is printed every
  run so the gate cannot quietly forget the exempt class exists.
