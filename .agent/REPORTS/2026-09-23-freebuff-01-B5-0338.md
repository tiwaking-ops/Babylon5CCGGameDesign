# B5-0338 — Aftermath targeting + D4 uniqueness (audit D2/D4)

**Agent:** Buffy (deepseek-v4-flash) · **javac:** 1.8.0_292 · **Scope:**
`b5ccg/src/b5ccg/model/`, `b5ccg/src/b5ccg/engine/` · **Claim:** live-held,
released at close.

## What landed

* **`model/AftermathCard`** — `isParticipantCondition()`: the trigger contains
  "PARTICIPANT", the rulebook key that widens targeting (Aftermath Cards /
  Participant).
* **`model/GameState`** — attached-aftermath registry keyed by target Player:
  `getAttachedAftermaths`, `canAttachAftermath`, `attachAftermath` enforcing
  the D4 rule ("only one of each named aftermath may be in play on the same
  target") keyed on the card TITLE. Empty in today's discard-after-effect
  flow; authoritative the moment persistent effects exist.
* **`engine/RulesEngine`** — 6-arg `canPlayAftermath`: the playing player's
  play conditions (Won/Lost/Participant, conflict type) are evaluated exactly
  as before; the D2 target rule is new — non-Participant aftermaths target
  only the conflict's initiator; Participant aftermaths may target any
  participant of the resolved conflict; D4 registry consulted when a state is
  supplied. The legacy 4-arg form delegates as self-target.
* **`engine/GameController`** — the live play site selects a deterministic
  target (Participant aftermaths self-target when the playing player was a
  participant — the common WON_/LOST_PARTICIPANT case — otherwise the
  initiator), checks legality through the 6-arg form, attaches to the
  registry, logs the target, and the effect lands on the TARGET.
* **`engine/HeadlessConformanceTest`** — new AMT section (14 checks): D2
  both directions (initiator-only for plain aftermaths; participant set for
  Participant aftermaths; outsiders rejected), play conditions staying with
  the playing player, legacy-form behavior, effect-on-target with registry
  evidence, D4 same-title refusal / different-title attach / other-target
  freedom / registry-through-legality, and the empty-registry baseline.

## Interpretations recorded (full text in DECISIONS.md)

* "Supported, Opposed or Attacked" is represented by the resolved conflict's
  participants — the model has no distinct attack state; the participant set
  is the natural extension point when attack semantics land.
* "Named" aftermath = card title; per-target uniqueness keyed on title.
* One deliberate behavior change: the legacy self-target form now applies the
  D2 rule, so a non-initiator playing a non-Participant aftermath on himself
  is refused ("aftermaths may normally be played only upon the faction that
  initiated"); one pre-existing D1 suite check was updated to target the
  initiator explicitly. The winning player's LOST-aftermath rights are
  unchanged.
