## The row's explicit question: per-path lines or a pattern?

**Answer: a name-scoped pattern, `/b5ccg/compile-*.log`.** Stated reasoning:

- The *narrower* alternative (one line per named file) is **provably rotted by the
  evidence**: three same-family logs appeared mid-session that per-path lines written
  at seed time would have missed — one of them a cited gate artifact. Per-path lines
  rot exactly as fast as the queue generates logs.
- The *broader* alternative (`/b5ccg/*.log`) is wider than the measured evidence
  supports and would silently capture any future hand-written or tool log under
  `b5ccg/` that a human might want to see; the name scope captures exactly the
  per-task gate-output family measured.
- Cross-check: no `*.log` exists anywhere in the tracked tree outside
  `.agent/SCOPE_RELEASES/` (run-queue dry-run captures, tracked deliberately, a
  different directory this rule does not touch — verified NOT-IGNORED).
