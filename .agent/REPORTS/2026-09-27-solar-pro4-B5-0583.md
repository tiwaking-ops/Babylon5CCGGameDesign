**Reusable lesson:** When a checkpoint commit excludes `.agent/CLAIMS/` per protocol, the claim files for the checkpoint's own tasks become orphaned on disk (or are deleted separately). A later verification pass that greps `git ls-files` for tracked-but-absent claim files will find them and must distinguish between (a) deletions that are the expected result of a prior checkpoint's CLAIMS/ exclusion (legitimate) and (b) deletions with no corresponding checkpoint or cleanup record (requires investigation).
