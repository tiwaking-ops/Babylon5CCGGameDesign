## What the task required

Stop the always-oppose default, score support vs oppose by conflict outcome, and
make HARD initiation prefer weaker targets over the auto-leader. The audit's
finding: the boolean `shouldJoinConflict` fed a hardcoded
`addParticipant(p, false)` in the controller — AI never supported a conflict,
even one it was sure to win.

## What landed (3 files)

- **`AIPlayer`** — new `decideJoinSide(GameState, Player, Conflict)` returning
  +1 (support) / −1 (oppose) / 0 (abstain); `shouldJoinConflict` delegates, so
  legacy callers are untouched.
  - **MEDIUM:** oppose when its total outweighs the initiator (opposition wins
    ties per B5-0309 resolution), support when below half (free-ride), abstain
    in the middle band (deliberate tightening over the old join-everything;
    recorded in DECISIONS).
  - **HARD:** as MEDIUM plus: never strengthens the leading player's conflict
    (oppose if it can outweigh, otherwise abstain); MILITARY joins respect the
    B5-0309 ambassador-damage rule (≥3 gap harms the loser's ambassador);
    initiation scoring flips the old `leaderBonus +3` to an anti-leader
    penalty, so HARD initiates against weak targets rather than entrenching
    the leader.
  - **EASY:** random side when joining, abstain half the time — preserves the
    historical ~50% join rate (an intermediate draft doubled it; caught before
    landing).
- **`GameController`** — the single join call site now commits to the chosen
  side (one-line engine extension, within the row's intent; logged).
- **`HeadlessConformanceTest`** — AIJ section, 16 checks: side-selection
  determinism, leader-never-strengthened, military-loss abstention band, EASY
  join-rate bound, legacy delegation, and a deterministic initiation-target
  check (weak target over the board leader).

## Design notes

- DIPLOMACY-based checks throughout: since B5-0337, `conflictTotal(MILITARY)`
  counts fleets only, so character-driven join logic is asserted on DIPLOMACY.
- The initiation check is deterministic by construction: the AI holds the only
  conflict card, both candidate targets have equal totals, so win-probability
  terms cancel and the anti-leader penalty decides.
