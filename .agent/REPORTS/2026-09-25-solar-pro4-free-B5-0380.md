Relabels to "Discard Agenda (Major)" and shows a Major-specific tooltip when a Major is installed.
- **replaceAgendaButton**: submits `GameAction.replaceAgenda((AgendaCard) selectedCard, leader)` gated by `rules.canReplaceAgenda(hp, (AgendaCard) selectedCard, leader)`. Requires a selected AgendaCard in hand and a ready unrotated Inner Circle leader. Tooltip explains Major-for-Major replacement.

- **refreshAgendaControls(Player human, boolean actionTurn)**: refreshes all three buttons from current state on every `refresh()` call. Also called from `updatePlayInitiateButtons()`.
- All three buttons are action-phase-only and require the human's turn.
