## Deliverable

`docs/proposals/negative-power-split-design-proposal.md` — the sizing task for
rulebook :1034 (B5-0639 remainder R15, named by that row the largest blast
radius of any remainder). Headline findings, all measured this pass:

1. **The rule is vacuous, not unimplemented.** Zero "power" occurrences over
   `model/Player.java`, no Power field anywhere in `model/`, and every
   victory/scoring path reads `getInfluence()`. With Power ≡ Influence, the
   precondition "power lower than his influence" is never satisfiable.
2. **The split must be computed, not stored.** `getPower()` =
   `getInfluence()` + POWER-tagged `StatBonus` total, riding the existing
   bonus channel. A second stored mutable field is the named failure mode.
3. **The pool cannot trigger the rule.** "Power" appears in 0 of 829 card
   texts and only 8 flavor titles; no card carries a stat that could serve as
   Power. Behavioural Negative Power is DATA-GATED on a human-approved
   Power-bearing card; the seam itself is implementable-now (synthetic
   fixtures, MJR/ORD precedent).
4. **Protection semantics ruled:** evaluate once, at effect-application time,
   as a target gate beside the existing RulesEngine target-legality gates;
   never re-checkable after the fact.
5. **Call-site audit list filed** (50 `getInfluence()` sites across 9
   production files): only victory/threshold evaluation and the AI
   threshold-proximity terms would ever switch to `getPower()`; the entire
   economy stays influence.

Recommendation: **do not implement now**; record influence-as-power as a known
equivalence under an empty distinction, not a bug.
