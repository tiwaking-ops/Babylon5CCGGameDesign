# B5-0703 — AI Power awareness (2026-09-28, solar-pro4:free)

## Summary

Taught MEDIUM and HARD to value POWER-tagged bonuses through Player.getPower() by editing the one AI helper that scores event timing by relative board position — `eventCatchUpBonus` — switching its "who is ahead and by how much" read from `getInfluence()` to `getPower()`. Zero-cost invariance preserved (no POWER bonus present → `getPower() == getInfluence()` byte-identically, so the pre-0703 AI ordering is unchanged). EASY untouched.

## What changed

- **One file, one helper:** `b5ccg/src/b5ccg/ai/AIPlayer.java` — `eventCatchUpBonus(GameState, Player)` (private). Two local variables (`myPower`, `qPower`) now cache `p.getPower()` / `q.getPower()` instead of the old `getInfluence()` reads. The helper is called by `scoreActionMedium` (event branch) and `scoreActionHard` (event branch); both MEDIUM and HARD event scoring now see Power.
- **No other AI paths touched.** `agendaProximityScore`, `leadingPlayer`, `majorProximityMedium/Hard`, `damageAttackScore`, every action-type scorer, `buildLegalActions`, `easyChoose`, `mediumChoose`, `hardChoose` — all unchanged. The AI's existing `getPower()` reads in those other paths (already present before this task) are not part of this task's scope and were not re-verified here; they are covered by the 643-suite.
- **No engine/model/ui/card-data edits. No conformance section edit.**

## Reusable lesson

When a derived quantity like Power retroactively changes the meaning of a value a downstream consumer already reads by name (here `getPower()` replaced `getInfluence()` in one helper), verify the zero-cost floor with a synthetic fixture that holds the bonus total at zero and asserts byte-identical equality — not just "the suite still passes", because the suite's PWR section already asserted the seam itself and would pass even if the AI helper had regressed to influence-only.
