# B5-0315 design proposal — card cost field + double-cost rule wiring (C1/D13)

## 1. What this proposal is (and is not)

**This is a design proposal only.** It records a planned schema + model + engine-wiring path for a card `cost` field and the rulebook's double-cost / promote-cost rules. **No JSON edit, no model edit, and no engine edit are made by this task.** The task ends at the proposal doc.

This proposal exists because three prior reports independently recorded the same gap:

- **B5-0203 D13** — "the model does not carry a card cost field."
- **B5-0305 D13** — "the double-influence-cost rule for sponsoring another race's loyal characters needs a card cost field the model does not carry."
- **B5-0311 C1 (P0/HIGH)** — "no card in either file (`premiere.json`, `deluxe.json`) carries a `cost` field; all card types, both files."

The rulebook defines influence cost as a primary card attribute (§Anatomy item 2: "Influence Cost: The amount of influence required to bring a card into play") and two actions that spend it: **Sponsor a Supporting Card** and **Promote a Character to the Inner Circle** (§V). The model today has no field for it; the engine has neither action implemented (Sponsor / Promote are both absent from `GameAction.Type`; Build Influence exists via B5-0301). So the gap is both a **data-schema gap** (no field in `cards/`) and a **missing-action gap** (Sponsor/Promote not in the engine). This proposal addresses the data+model side only; the missing actions are flagged as a dependency, not implemented here.

## 2. Exact rulebook cost rules this field must support

### 2.1 The core definition

Rulebook §Anatomy of a Card, item 2:

> Influence Cost: The amount of influence required to bring a card into play.

Rulebook §Influence:

> Each player has an Influence Rating... During each turn, players may apply influence (up to the total of their Influence Ratings) as a means to further their aims. Each point of Influence Rating also counts equally towards the Power total... Influence applied during a player's turn is restored at the beginning of the following turn.

So "influence cost" is a scalar that a player must **apply from their available influence pool** to bring a card into play. Today's model has `Player.influence` as a single merged number and no "applied vs available" split, and no card-side cost to apply against. (This proposal does not redesign the pool; it assumes the existing `Player.influence` semantics stay and a new action would consult `cost` against it.)

### 2.2 Sponsor a Supporting Card (§V)

Rulebook §V "ACTION: Sponsor a Supporting Card":

> Any character in the Inner Circle may rotate to bring a new supporting Character, Enhancement, Group, Location or Fleet into play from your hand. The new supporting card is placed in the Supporting Cards Row, ready to act on the same turn. Your faction must apply the required influence cost listed on the sponsored card being brought into play or this action may not be performed.

And the race-type clause:

> Characters with a different race name written as part of their card type require a faction to apply double the listed influence cost to sponsor. Neutral characters may be sponsored by any race at no additional influence cost.
> ...
> Any other type of card with a race name as part of its card type is restricted to that race; i.e., only the player controlling that race may play such cards.

So the field must support (a) a base printed cost, (b) a double-for-other-race multiplier for **characters**, and (c) a **restricted-to-race** concept for race-named types across types (characters, enhancements, other types). The rulebook's restriction text is type-generic ("Any other type of card with a race name as part of its card type is restricted to that race"), not character-only.

### 2.3 Promote a Character to the Inner Circle (§V)

Rulebook §V "ACTION: Promote a Character to the Inner Circle":

> To successfully promote a supporting character, a faction must apply influence equal to the supporting character's influence cost (again, doubled if the character is loyal to a different race) plus one additional influence for each character that is already a member of your faction's Inner Circle. E.g., if your faction has an ambassador and one other member in its Inner circle, then to promote a character with an influence cost of three would require a total of five influence to be applied. Note that a discount to the cost to sponsor a character does not apply to promoting the character unless specifically stated otherwise.

So promote cost = `card.cost` (+ double if other-race loyal) `+ (innerCircleCount)` where innerCircleCount includes the ambassador (the ambassador is always in the Inner Circle). Note the explicit statement that **sponsor discounts do not automatically apply to promote** unless the card says so — so the wiring must treat sponsor cost and promote cost as separately computable, not just "same number."

### 2.4 Build Influence (§V) — for context, not a cost field consumer

> Any faction with an Influence Rating of 9 or less may increase its Influence Rating by 1 by rotating a member of its Inner Circle and applying 3 influence... Factions with an Influence Rating of 10 or greater may not use this action.

Build Influence is **already implemented** (B5-0301: `BUILD_INFLUENCE` action, AI offers at rating ≤ 9). It applies a fixed 3 influence, **not** a per-card cost. So Build Influence does **not** consume a `cost` field. This proposal does not touch B5-0301.

## 3. Proposed JSON schema extension

### 3.1 Field name, type, placement

- Field name: **`cost`** (matches the rulebook term "influence cost" and the B5-0311 finding's naming).
- Type: **non-negative integer** (`0`+).
- Placement: **top-level key on every card object that can be brought into play by spending influence**, i.e. the cards the Sponsor/Promote actions actually target.

### 3.2 Which card types get the field

Sponsor a Supporting Card (§V) brings into play: **Character, Enhancement, Group, Location, Fleet** (from hand, into the Supporting Cards Row). It does **not** bring into play: Aftermath, Conflict, Agenda (agenda has its own "Discard, Replace, Reveal or Sponsor an Agenda" action with its own cost structure — see §3.4), Event/Contingency (played for effect, not "sponsored" as a permanent).

So the field should be present on:

- **Character** — yes (sponsored into supporting row; promotable to IC; double-cost applies).
- **Fleet** — yes (sponsored into supporting row; restricted to race if race-named; cannot promote).
- **Location** — yes (sponsored into supporting row; restricted to race if race-named; cannot promote).
- **Enhancement** — yes (sponsored into play; restricted to race if race-named; placement row depends on target).
- **Group** — yes (sponsored into supporting row; restricted to race if race-named; cannot promote).

And **not** on (proposal: omit, or allow optional with no current consumer):

- **Aftermath** — aftermaths are played **during a conflict**, not sponsored from hand into the supporting row. The field is not consumed by any current action. Proposal: omit (keep file lean); if a future action wants it, add then.
- **Conflict** — conflicts are initiated, not sponsored; the field would have no consumer. Omit.
- **Agenda** — agenda has "Discard, Replace, Reveal or Sponsor an Agenda" (§V), which is a separate sponsorship path with its own cost semantics ("bring a new agenda into play requires you to rotate a character of your Inner Circle and is also considered to be an action"). The rulebook does not state a printed per-agenda influence cost in the same way as supporting cards. Proposal: omit for now; if an agenda cost is wanted, treat it as a separate design sub-problem (not part of this proposal's scope).
- **Event / Contingency** — played for effect, no printed sponsorship cost in the rulebook sense. Omit.

### 3.3 Default / backward-compat stance

Because **no card in either file currently has `cost`**, adding the field is a **forward-compat addition**: old cards simply lack the key. Any loader/reader that consults `cost` must treat **missing `cost` as a defined default** (proposal: `0` for "no cost printed" — meaning free to bring into play — NOT as an error), so the existing 448-premiere + deluxe cards keep loading without per-card edits.

This is the critical backward-compat decision: **do not require the field on every card now.** Require the *reader* to handle absence. That keeps B5-0305's "data has no field" gap documented while letting a future implementer add costs card-by-card without a mass-edit.

### 3.4 Deluxe handling

`deluxe.json` reuses premiere card text with deluxe variants (B5-0311 observation). Proposal: **deluxe cards get their own `cost` if/when costs are assigned**, decided per card from rulebook text / usable reference — not auto-copied from the premiere counterpart. Costs are a printed-card attribute; if a deluxe card's text differs, its cost may differ. No auto-derivation rule is proposed here because there is no usable source for the actual numbers yet (B5 IP-safe rules forbid copying card text — see §6).

### 3.5 Example shape (proposal only, no edit)

A character that today reads (paraphrased from `premiere.json` structure):

```json
{"id":"char_example","title":"Example Character","type":"CHARACTER",
 "subtype":"CHARACTER_HUMAN","loyalty":"HUMAN","rarity":"COMMON","faction":"HUMAN",
 "abilities":{"diplomacy":4,"intrigue":2,"psi":0,"leadership":1},
 "imageKey":"example", ...}
```

would, **when a cost is assigned**, gain a top-level `"cost": <int>` key. No other key changes. The loader already reads arbitrary top-level keys; adding one is non-breaking if the reader defaults absent → 0.

## 4. Proposed model-surface extension

### 4.1 Where the field lives

Proposal: **a new method on the shared card base type** (the type that `CharacterCard`, `FleetCard`, `LocationCard`, `EnhancementCard`, `GroupCard` all extend — the same base the loader hydrates), e.g.:

- `int getCost()` — returns the printed influence cost, default 0 when absent.

And **not** on `ConflictCard`, `AftermathCard`, or `AgendaCard` (no current consumer; keep them out of the contract until an action needs them).

### 4.2 Loading defaults

The loader (`DeckLoader` / whatever hydrates card JSON into model objects) should map a missing `"cost"` key to **0** (not fail). This is the backward-compat hinge: existing cards without the key load as cost 0.

If the loader today stores the JSON object as a `Map`/`JsonObject`/similar and the model accesses fields by key, then `getCost()` is "read int at key `cost`, default 0" — minimal. If the model instead uses typed fields, the field needs a Java-level default ( proposal: `private int cost = 0;` won't work for "absent vs present 0" distinction — but for this proposal's purposes, absent and explicit-0 are both "no cost to apply," so a plain defaulting field is acceptable; a stricter design would track presence separately, which is out of scope here).

### 4.3 The double-cost / restriction decision is **Faction-side**, not card-side

B5-0305 already fixed `Faction.isPlayableBy` (D13): NON_ALIGNED is now a normal race, NEUTRAL universal, ANY unchanged. The double-cost / restricted-to-race decision for Sponsor/Promote is **a function of (card race/type, faction race)**, not a card-only property:

- "Characters with a different race name... require double the listed influence cost to sponsor." → needs card's loyalty/race vs sponsor's faction race.
- "Restricted Enhancements may only be sponsored by a character of the listed race, and may only enhance a card of that same race." → restriction check, not just cost.
- "Any other type of card with a race name as part of its card type is restricted to that race." → restriction check per type.

So the model should expose **cost** (the scalar) and let the action/ engine compute the **effective cost** and the **playability/restriction** from `Faction` + card type + card race. Do not push "is this card double-cost for me" into the card object.

### 4.4 What the model should NOT do in this proposal

- Do not add a "double cost" boolean to the card. The doubling is a per-action, per-faction computation.
- Do not tie cost to `Player.influence` spending here (that's the missing-action gap, §5).
- Do not add an `effectiveCost(faction)` method that also encodes restriction — keep restriction and cost separate (restriction can block the action entirely; cost is what is applied if allowed).

## 5. Proposed engine wiring plan (dependencies, not implementation)

### 5.1 What is missing before `cost` has any effect

Today, neither **Sponsor a Supporting Card** nor **Promote a Character to the Inner Circle** exists as a `GameAction.Type`, and neither is wired in `GameController.processAction` or `RulesEngine`. So adding a `cost` field now has **zero runtime effect until those actions exist**. This proposal records that dependency explicitly; it does not create the actions.

### 5.2 Where cost would be consulted once the actions exist

**Sponsor a Supporting Card** would need, at minimum:

1. Target = a supporting card in hand (Character/Enhancement/Group/Location/Fleet).
2. rotatingInnerCircleCharacter = an IC character (the sponsor) — already a concept from B5-0301's Build Influence rotation semantics.
3. **Affordability check**: `player.getAvailableInfluence() >= effectiveCost(card, player.faction)`, where `effectiveCost` = `card.getCost()` doubled if card loyalty race ≠ faction race (for characters) and/or restricted-to-race block (per type), else `card.getCost()`. **This is where `cost` first becomes meaningful.**
4. On success: place card in Supporting Cards Row, apply the influence (deduct from available pool / mark as applied), ready to act same turn.
5. On failure: action not legal (cannot afford) or not allowed (restricted race) — card stays in hand.

**Promote a Character to the Inner Circle** would need:

1. Target = a ready supporting **character** (not fleet/location/group — those cannot promote per rulebook).
2. rotatingInnerCircleCharacter = sponsor.
3. **Affordability check**: `player.getAvailableInfluence() >= card.getCost() (+ double if other-race loyal) + innerCircleCount`, where `innerCircleCount` = current IC size (ambassador counts).
4. On success: move card from supporting row to IC row; apply influence; note that sponsor discounts do not apply unless the card says so (so the formula uses raw `cost`, not any discounted sponsor cost).

### 5.3 Influence pool semantics (open question, recorded here)

The rulebook says influence is "applied... up to the total of their Influence Ratings" and "restored at the beginning of the following turn." Today's `Player.influence` is a **single number** (B5-0203 noted `spendInfluence` exists but no restore-to-rating cycle; `Player.influence` is one merged number). So a Sponsor/Promote action would need to define:

- Is there a separate "available this turn" pool vs "rating" pool? Or is `Player.influence` the pool and applying cost subtracts from it, restored at turn reset?
- Does Build Influence's "apply 3 influence" already model the apply/restore cycle, and do Sponsor/Promote share it?

This proposal does not resolve that — it flags it as a **prerequisite design question** for whoever implements Sponsor/Promote. The `cost` field is independent of that question (the field is just the printed number); the pool semantics decide how `cost` is paid.

### 5.4 Double-cost for "loyal to a different race" — definitional dependency

The rulebook phrase is "Characters with a different race name written as part of their card type." The model has `Faction` (race) and cards have a loyalty/race attribute. B5-0305 already touched `Faction.isPlayableBy` for NON_ALIGNED. The double-cost rule needs a clear definition of "card's race" for each type that can be sponsored — proposal: use the card's loyalty/race field when present (characters), and for race-named type cards (enhancements, groups, locations, fleets with a race in the type), use that type race. This is a small definitional sub-point that whoever implements Sponsor/Promote should pin down in `docs/DECISIONS.md`; not resolved here.

## 6. IP-safe constraint on cost values

B5 IP-safe rules forbid copying card text, images, or lore dumps. Printed influence costs are part of the printed cards. So **this proposal cannot supply actual numeric cost values for specific cards** — there is no source the project is allowed to use for that. Any future mass cost assignment would need a source the project is allowed to use (e.g. a project-authorized reference, original design values, or per-card human-provided values). This proposal therefore proposes the **schema and wiring**, not the **data population**. Populating `cost` values is a separate (likely multi-card, data-edit) task that should not be opened until a usable source exists.

## 7. What this proposal recommends doing now (and what it recommends NOT doing now)

### 7.1 Recommend: accept the schema as the target state

- Add `cost` (non-negative int, default 0 when absent) to the card base type that the loader hydrates.
- Make the loader map missing `"cost"` → 0.
- Expose `getCost()` on the types Sponsor/Promote target (Character, Enhancement, Group, Location, Fleet); do not add it to Conflict/Aftermath/Agenda today.

This is small, backward-compatible, and closes the "data has no field" gap on the model side (B5-0305's explicit remainder) without touching any JSON or any runtime behavior.

### 7.2 Recommend: record the missing-action dependency

- Sponsor a Supporting Card and Promote a Character to the Inner Circle are **not implemented**. The cost field is a prerequisite for them, not a substitute.
- Whoever implements those actions should record the effective-cost formula, the race-restriction rule, and the influence-pool semantics in `docs/DECISIONS.md` before wiring.

### 7.3 Recommend: do NOT mass-assign cost values now

- No usable IP-safe source for per-card printed costs exists for this project. Opening a task to fill in `cost` on existing cards would either stall (no values) or risk IP unsafe copying. Recommend leaving data population unopened until a source is available.

### 7.4 Recommend: do NOT redesign the influence pool in this task

- The pool semantics (available vs rating, apply/restore) is a separate design question. This proposal keeps `cost` orthogonal to it.

## 8. Open questions (for whoever implements Sponsor/Promote later)

1. **Influence pool model.** Is there a separate "available this turn" influence pool, or does `Player.influence` serve as the pool with apply/restore at turn boundary? Does Build Influence (B5-0301) already define the cycle Sponsor/Promote should share?
2. **Card "race" for non-character sponsored types.** For a race-named enhancement/group/location/fleet, what attribute carries "the race name written as part of the card type" for the double-cost / restriction check — the subtype string, a loyalty field, or a dedicated field? (Characters have loyalty; other types may not.)
3. **Agenda sponsorship cost.** Rulebook "Discard, Replace, Reveal or Sponsor an Agenda" is its own action; does an agenda have a printed influence cost, or is its cost the rotation+IC-slot semantics? If agenda cost is wanted, it is a separate sub-design from supporting-card cost.
4. **Default 0 meaning.** Is "no cost field / cost 0" intended to mean "free to bring into play," or should absent cost mean "cost not yet defined" (and therefore Sponsor/Promote should not offer the card until a cost is set)? Recommendation in §3.3 is "absent = 0 = free," which is backward-compatible; the alternative (absent = not offerable) would block all existing cards until costs are assigned, which this proposal advises against.
5. **Deluxe vs premiere cost divergence.** When a deluxe card's cost differs from its premiere counterpart, that is fine (§3.4) — but it means costs are per-card, not per-logical-card. Future auditors should not assume deluxe.cost == premiere.cost.
