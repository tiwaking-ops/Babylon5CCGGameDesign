## Interpretations recorded (DECISIONS B5-0340)

1. **Eligibility = not barred (correction to B5-0354's first reading).**
   Condition 2's "one player eligible to win a standard victory" was
   initially interpreted in the B5-0354 report as "satisfies the condition-1
   20-Power bar" — which makes condition 2 vacuous (condition 1 always fires
   first for such a player). Corrected per rulebook :170 ("At the start of
   game play, each player is eligible to win by scoring a Standard
   Victory"): eligible = not barred, i.e. no major agenda (rulebook :178)
   and no Shadow War. The B5-0354 report carries a dated assessor note for
   the correction.
2. **"End of a turn" = the round boundary.** `checkVictory` already runs
   there after every action round; this engine's turn unit is the round
   (consistent with B5-0302's per-turn conflict mapping).
3. **Shadow/Vorlon ratings now exist as state.** B5-0354 scoped them out of
   the minimal entity, but the Shadow-War guard needs a canonical trigger
   surface; storing the two ints beside the station (clamped, unmoved by
   anything today) keeps `isShadowWar()` honest without building Shadow-War
   mechanics (a future task).
4. **Start at 0, no drift.** Per B5-0354: the conservative default keeps
   condition 2 provably inert in today's games (suite check 2 proves the
   19-station case never fires) until real station-influence sources land.
