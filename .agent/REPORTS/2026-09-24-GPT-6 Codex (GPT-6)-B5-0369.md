# B5-0369 — Rating and applied influence pool

Split each player's permanent Influence Rating from the per-turn applied pool. The pool starts at Rating, application consumes only the pool, permanent gains/losses update Rating and the current pool together, and `startRound()` restores the pool to Rating. Recruitment, promotion, and Build Influence affordability now read the pool. Build Influence applies three and permanently raises Rating by one; victory and AI positional scoring continue to read Rating.

Following the proposal's D9.3 interpretation, a permanent +1 Rating during a Build Influence action also adds one point to the current pool. Thus from Rating/pool 9/9, the action applies three (pool 6), then the permanent gain produces Rating/pool 10/7. The permanent Rating is the Power contribution.
