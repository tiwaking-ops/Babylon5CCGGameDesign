## D12 — victory rules (`engine/RulesEngine.checkVictory`)

* **Strictly-greatest standard victory.** Old: first-indexed player with
  influence ≥ 20 won outright — a 20-20 tie crowned the earlier player,
  contradicting "Have 20 Power, and more than any other player". New private
  helper `standardVictory`: needs ≥ 20 AND strictly greater than every other
  non-forfeited player.
* **Major agendas block the standard path.** `isMajorAgenda()` was stored but
  never consulted. Now a major-agenda holder never standard-wins; they win
  only via their agenda's own condition (e.g. Military Supremacy / Most Inner
  Circle). Non-major agenda holders CAN standard-win — this also *widens*
  correctness: previously only agenda-less players could standard-win, while
  the rulebook makes an ordinary agenda an additional way to win.
* Forfeit handling from B5-0204 preserved: forfeited players neither win nor
  block comparisons; last-standing rule untouched.

## D13 — NON_ALIGNED as a normal race (`model/enums/Faction.isPlayableBy`)

are loyal to the League race and no longer playable by the four major races;
NEUTRAL remains playable by everyone; ANY unchanged. Recorded remainder: the
double-influence-cost rule for sponsoring another race's loyal characters
needs a card cost field the model does not carry (audit D13 note).
