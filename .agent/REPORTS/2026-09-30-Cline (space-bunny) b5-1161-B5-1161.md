## Reusable lesson

A checklist row's premise is a claim about the ledger like any other, so verify
the premise's statuses, not just the row's stated gate: "three rows describe one
repeatable shape" can be one `DONE` and two `BLOCKED`, and the checklist's
hardest question may then have zero instances to generalise from. Corollary - a
predecessor's *report file existing* is not its close-out having landed; `DONE`
is the predicate, because a `BLOCKED` pass also files a report.
