## What landed (`GameBoardPanel.java` only)

1. **Conflict outcome banner.** The controller clears the active conflict
   immediately after resolution, so the panel *holds* the last `Conflict`
   object in `update()`: after `resolve(winner)` it retains
   `isResolved()` / `getWinner()` / side totals — structured outcome data,
   no log parsing. A green banner (`WON BY <player>` + support/opposition
   totals + influence reward) renders when the active conflict is gone,
   until a new conflict activates. Cleared-without-resolution (round
   advance) drops the held conflict.
2. **Agenda slot state.** Per player: `Agenda (MAJOR|minor): <title>` in
   gold, with a green `[WIN]` marker when `isConditionMet(state, p)` is
   true.
3. **Assistant markers** (B5-0339 surfaces): `A+` over the ambassador
   mini-card while the assist bonus is live, `A$` while a sponsor discount
   is pending.
4. **Attached-aftermaths line** (B5-0338 registry): count + up to three
   titles, snapshotted before iteration (the registry getter returns the
   live internal list — B5-0346 paint-thread lesson applied).

All read-side: no model or engine changes.

## Two real bugs the probe caught (both fixed)

1. **Banner occlusion.** The new indicator lines initially sat at `y+268`,
   *under* the center banner rectangle (`x w/4..3w/4`, `y 262..296`,
   drawn after the zones) — zone-2+ players' agenda lines were invisible
   exactly while a conflict was up, the moment the information matters
   most. Diagnosed by pixel-histogram (lines present in a no-conflict
   render, absent with the banner up). Lines moved below the banner band.
2. **Unreachable outcome banner.** The outcome-banner block was initially
   nested *inside* the `if (activeConflict != null)` block (a brace-fix
   artifact), so it could only draw while a conflict was active — the
   opposite of its purpose. Reflection + pixel diagnostic isolated capture
   (worked: title field set) from render (drew nothing); the block is now
   a sibling of the active-conflict block.
