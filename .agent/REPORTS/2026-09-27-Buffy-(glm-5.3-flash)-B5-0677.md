## Delivered (exactly the B5-0667 proposal §3.2/§3.4)

1. **`StatKey.POWER`** (model/enums/StatKey.java) — documented as a
   player-level add-on, never a card stat.
2. **`Player.getPower()`** — computed: `influence + getPowerBonusTotal()`,
   the sum of POWER-tagged `StatBonus` deltas. No stored field. With no POWER
   bonus present it equals `getInfluence()` exactly (the B5-0667 measured
   vacuity preserved byte-identically). Rides the existing bonus channel:
   `grantBonus` same-source replacement, `removeBonusesBySource`,
   `sweepBonusExpiries` all clear POWER add-ons without new machinery.
3. **`RulesEngine.canAffectTarget(Card, Player)`** — the rulebook :1034
   Negative Power gate: refuses an influence-as-power effect on a target
   whose `getPower() < getInfluence()`. Evaluated once at
   effect-application time, beside the other target-legality gates; never
   re-checkable after the fact. Returns true for null inputs (no source, no
   target — nothing to protect against).
4. **Economy untouched** — zero call sites switched to `getPower()`
   (checked: every victory/scoring path still reads `getInfluence()`).

## PWR conformance section (10 checks, all synthetic fixtures)

A: no POWER bonus ⇒ `getPower() == getInfluence()`. B: POWER-tagged +3 raises
Power above influence. C: a DIPLOMACY bonus does not move Power. D: negative
POWER bonus pushes Power below influence and the gate refuses the effect.
E: gate admits Power == influence and re-admits after the penalty source is
removed. F: influence gains do not alter the bonus total; Power tracks the
influence change (computed, not stored). G: source removal clears the add-on
via the shared channel. One expectation bug self-caught pre-compile: the
original check G used `sweepBonusExpiries`, which does not remove
`WHILE_IN_PLAY` bonuses — corrected to `removeBonusesBySource` (the actual
discard-path removal) before the first build.
