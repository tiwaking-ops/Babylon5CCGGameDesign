# A defect report that cannot be acted on trains the reader to ignore defect reports

Traces to: B5-1061 (content-pipe disposition policy).

**One-line lesson:** a detector that must keep reporting a defect class no
agent may repair is manufacturing noise on a schedule — the fix is not to
stop reporting but to let the artifact carry the adjudication, so the channel
distinguishes "adjudicated, keep" from "unexamined."

## The shape

- Nine ledger rows will read 8–15 pipes forever: the pipes are inside quoted
  content, repair would falsify the historical record, and no agent is
  authorised. Every census re-flags them; B5-1000 already spent a session
  re-adjudicating the same rows once; this session paid the tax again in
  every detector run.
- The danger is concrete: this very census *also* surfaced B5-1022's
  doubleLead — a real, actionable structural defect — as one row in a list of
  ten. A reader habituated to the list skims the one that matters. Alert
  fatigue is not a feeling here; it is a measured 10:1 noise ratio sitting on
  top of a 1-of-10 signal.
- The three standard answers each have a distinct failure mode: the
  exemption list hides future regressions on exempted rows, permanent noise
  taxes every census forever, and only the in-row marker keeps truth
  local — the artifact itself records "adjudicated, keep, reason," and any
  genuine edit destroys the marker automatically.

## What worked

- Cost each option in the currency already spent (two adjudication sessions)
  rather than in lines of code — the build costs are all trivial; the
  difference is who pays per census, forever.
- Keep the adjudication token *inside* the existing cell so the 7-pipe gate
  is untouched — the policy must not require violating the very invariant it
  manages.
- Exclude the structurally-repairable row from the marker class and name its
  owner, so the policy cannot become an excuse for B5-1039's real repair.

**Reusable lesson:** budget signal-to-noise as a real cost — a channel that
cannot silence its permanently-acknowledged findings will eventually bury a
live one, and the acknowledgment belongs in the artifact, not in the reader's
memory.
