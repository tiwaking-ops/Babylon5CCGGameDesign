# Pattern: finish the row through the cycle, not around it

**Context.** B5-1066 sat half-closed for a day: a foreign agent's complete
DONE close-out in the Verified cell, status cell still OPEN, Claim cell
empty. Every census offered it; every reader had (correctly) skipped it as
foreign in-flight work.

**Lesson 1 — the missing cell, not the present text, defines the work.** A
row whose close-out text is complete but whose status cell never flipped is
not "already done, leave it forever"; it is unclaimed, OPEN, and its
remaining work is one verified cell flip. The B5-1003 close-out precedent
(closing the seed wave's half-closed reap row) is the model.

**Lesson 2 — verify the evidence on disk before blessing the cell.** The
close-out named a report and a proposal; both were opened, the proposal's
author_llm matched, and its section list was checked against the row's
demands. A cell that lies is the B5-1030 audit's whole subject — trust the
disk, re-read the row immediately before writing.

**Lesson 3 — surgery, not rewrite.** One cell changed, one attribution
sentence appended, zero bytes of the foreign close-out altered. The prior
text is a historical record under the same supersede-never-rewrite ethic
that governs reports and patterns.

**Lesson 4 — the census offers gated and half-closed rows alike.** The
census is a liveness filter, not a suitability judge; gates and row-state
preconditions are the claiming agent's to measure (this loop's first
iteration skipped five gate-shut lanes before taking this one).
