# Pattern: citable beats findable

**Context.** B5-1098 hunted a printed cost the whole open web does not
carry (name/rarity lists only). The one value-bearing source was in-repo: a
scan transcription whose reader had just measured 36/36 against known
enhancement orbs and explicitly anti-verified the exact reading in
question.

**Lesson 1 — grade a source by measured accuracy, not by how far it
travelled.** A usenet list and a wiki carry rarity tables; a local
transcriber with a perfect score on 36 siblings carries the bubble value.
The web's coverage of Babylon 5 CCG is name-level; artifact-level data
lives where the scans live.

**Lesson 2 — single-source is a confidence label, not a rejection.** The
row forbade inventing values, not reporting single-sourced ones. State the
value, the chain, the confidence, and the cheapest upgrade path (a second
independent scan-read, or a human with the physical card) — and leave the
write decision with the human, exactly as the row scoped it.

**Lesson 3 — read the prior adjudications before hunting.** B5-0996 had
already classified this exact record Class D ("cost genuinely absent") and
DECISIONS carried the per-class receipt; the hunt was for a *value*, not
for whether one should exist.
