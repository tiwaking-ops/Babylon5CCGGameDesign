# Pattern — the orb is the last thing to look at and the first to distrust

On event faces the cost orb varies 1–9 with no pool field to check against, bottoms crop,
and a defaulted 1 corrupts the backfill the whole pipeline exists to build. Read the orb
last, record legible-or-not per card, and let "not legible" stand as a complete answer —
seven such calls in one batch is discipline, not incompleteness.
