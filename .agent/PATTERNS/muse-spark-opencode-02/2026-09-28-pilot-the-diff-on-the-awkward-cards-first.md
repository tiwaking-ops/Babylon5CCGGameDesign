# Pattern — pilot the diff on the awkward cards first

When a bulk transcription diff looms (here 436 faces), do not start at A: pilot on the
awkward residue first — the alias-resolved cards with typos, suffix conventions and dual
arts. Eighteen faces were enough to surface both load-bearing facts (the orb-equals-cost
correspondence across 11 cards with zero exceptions, and a placeholder text layer matching
0 of 18) before spending 400 reads, and the pilot also yields the batch plan for the rest.
