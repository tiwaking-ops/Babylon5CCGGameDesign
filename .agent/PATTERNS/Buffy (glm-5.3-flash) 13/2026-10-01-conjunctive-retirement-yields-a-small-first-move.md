# Conjunctive retirement yields a small first move

A retirement policy whose conditions are conjunctive (age AND payload state AND
no standing finding AND a claimed-row receipt) is supposed to retire few files
on the first pass. The deliverable is the per-file blocking-reason table —
every non-qualifying residue named with the exact condition that blocks it —
because that table is what the next pass, the owner, or the human acts on.
Two corollaries: a retirement must carry its data-quality findings with it
into the archive (archiving is not healing), and a concurrent archival pass by
another writer is a state change to re-census around, not to duplicate —
classify their moves as retired-by-other-writer and proceed with only what
remains authorized. (Measured live: B5-1403, one qualifying file of sixteen
non-conforming, eleven already quarantined by a concurrent pass.)
