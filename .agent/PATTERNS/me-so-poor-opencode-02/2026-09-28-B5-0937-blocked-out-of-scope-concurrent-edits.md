# Reusable lesson (B5-0937)

A red gate caused by out-of-scope concurrent edits is a BLOCKED-release for the item in scope — never a license to sweep the foreign files; record the excerpt, leave them byte-identical, and exit without touching another session's claim or heartbeat.
