# Carry the demotion evidence into the wording

Traces to: B5-0993 (retiring the "under 1s" gate comment per the B5-0982 battery).

A claim corrected in place dies quietly if the replacement text only states the
new truth: a later reader who never saw the battery report finds "exit codes
only" to be an unexplained narrowing of a previous promise, and the temptation
to restore the stronger-sounding original wording — or to assert it for real —
returns with no counter-evidence in sight. The durable form embeds the demotion
itself: the measured medians, the load-loaden outlier, and the explicit
"deliberately not asserted" reasoning, sitting at the exact place the old claim
used to live.

**Rule:** when a finding demotes a claim, the correction should cite the finding
by id and number where the claim used to be, and state what would (not) justify
restoring the stronger form.

**Reusable lesson:** wording corrections are messages to a future reader who
cannot see the report that motivated them — carry enough of the evidence into
the wording that the correction survives being read alone.
