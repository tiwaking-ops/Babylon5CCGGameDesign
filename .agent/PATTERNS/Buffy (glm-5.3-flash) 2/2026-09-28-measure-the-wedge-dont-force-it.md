# Measure the wedge, don't force it

Traces to: B5-0997 (three-signal re-measurement of the B5-0953 ghost), after
B5-0963 and B5-0996 reached the same wedge from other angles.

A stuck lock behind an ownership rule produces a specific temptation chain: the
claim is provably dead, the gates behind it are provably blocked, and each new
measurement session re-proves both. The temptation is to let the proof graduate
into action — delete the file "since everyone agrees". The rule that holds is
that agreement is not authorization: the claim file belongs to its owner, the
reap requires an authorized actor, and a measurement row that deleted the lock
would convert its own scope violation into the precedent the next impatient
agent cites. The correct product of a measurement-only row is therefore a
*reap-ready evidence package*: signals with ages taken against one wall-clock
reading, the liveness-rule fallback that defeats the forged payload, the exact
deletion that opens the gates, and the observation that the owning session is
dead by its own heartbeat contract (976 minutes of silence against a 5-minute
refresh discipline).

**Rule:** when a wedge repeats across sessions, the deliverable is not another
verdict — it is the evidence package that makes the authorized fix a one-line
action, plus the discipline to stop at measurement because the scope says so.

**Reusable lesson:** deadlocks are broken by authorization, not by accumulation
of proof — but proof written reap-ready is what lets the authorized actor act
immediately instead of re-measuring the same three signals a fourth time.
