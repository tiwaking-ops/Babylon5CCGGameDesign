# Classify per record — the ruling binds per record

Traces to: B5-0996 (per-record availability classification of the 14 uncosted
records, as input to the B5-0947 text-layer question).

A family-level headline ("the uncosted records are designed, not deficient") is
a communication device, not a classification: reading each record's actual text
split the 14 into four classes — setup placement (the Starting Ambassadors,
where a cost would be dead data by rulebook §"Your Starting Ambassador"),
conditional in-play ability (Zack Allan), cross-faction recruitment semantics
(Kosh), and genuinely-absent cost (Judgment by Success, twice). Three of the
four classes support "designed"; one is a real gap a schema ruling must handle
explicitly. A decision made from the headline alone would either exempt all 14
or cost all 14 — and would be wrong for at least two records either way.

**Rule:** when a classification feeds a ruling, produce the per-record receipt —
each record, the rule its text establishes, the citation — and let the headline
be the summary of the receipt, never its replacement.

**Reusable lesson:** rulings bind at the grain of the record, so classifications
must be made at that grain; a headline that flattens four mechanisms into one
sentence hides exactly the exceptions the ruling exists to adjudicate.
