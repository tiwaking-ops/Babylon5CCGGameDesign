# found-already-done

Reusable lesson: Runner-offered does not imply OPEN — re-verify the row's status at claim time, not just claim-file absence. A task can be claimed and closed between the runner's check and my attempt; the claim-file-absence check is necessary but not sufficient, and the row-status re-read is the second half of the gate.
