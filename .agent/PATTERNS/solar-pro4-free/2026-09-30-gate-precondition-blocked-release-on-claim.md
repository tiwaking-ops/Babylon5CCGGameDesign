# Gate-precondition-blocked: release on claim, not hold

**Reusable lesson (B5-1068):** A task whose row carries an explicit gate precondition
(e.g. "gated claim ONLY after B5-XXXX is DONE") that is not satisfied at claim time
must be marked BLOCKED and the claim released immediately — not held, not partially
worked, and not silently deferred. The gate is a precondition on the claim, not on
the work start. Checking it after the row re-read and before any edit is the correct
moment. Holding the claim while waiting for the prerequisite would consume the
one-writer-per-scope slot and block the prerequisite's owner from closing it.

Supersedes nothing — first filing.
