# B5-0428 — Station-influence movement design (proposal)

*`author_llm: Solar Pro4 (solar-pro4:free)`*

## What

Proposal-only design for a continuous per-station influence model (B5-0340 recorded station plus shadow/vorlon ratings, inert from day one). Sources, sinks, end-of-round condition-2 checks, Shadow-War trigger wiring, and tension-matrix interaction. No src/ or resources/ edits; no Support Babylon 5 player-side rewiring (B5-0354 trap). Read in-tree API surface only (B5-0340/B5-0376).

## Why now

B5-0427 and B5-0429 will expose captured-location and station/tension readout on the MainWindow toolbar (ui/ readout only). Those reads need a model to read from. B5-0340 already records a Babylon5Station plus a tension matrix in GameState, but nothing moves the three station ratings (human, shadow, vorlon) from round to round. This proposal defines that motion so the readout tasks have something to display and the GSS-measurable design-intent class has a target behavior.

## In-tree API surface read (B5-0340 / B5-0376)

- GameState holds the Babylon5Station (B5-0340) — a recorded station the players are collectively tied to.
- GameState holds the tension matrix (B5-0376) — per-faction tension numbers, 0-10, derived from board state.
- LocationCard carries `capturedBy` plus `effectsSuppressed` (B5-0376 Phase B) — engine-live, UI-invisible before B5-0427.
- The ui/ grep for "station" is empty; the only war surface today is the WAR banner plus the declare-war control.

No engine or model edits proposed here. This is a design proposal: what the ratings are, what moves them, and how the readout tasks (B5-0427/B5-0429) would consume them.

## Ratings

Three per-station continuous ratings, 0-100, recorded on the Babylon5Station in GameState:

| Rating | Owner | Inert from day one? | Intent |
|--------|-------|---------------------|--------|
| Human influence | human players (collective) | yes | measures how strongly the human side controls/anchors the station |
| Shadow influence | shadow faction | yes | measures shadow reach at the station |
| Vorlon influence | vorlon faction | yes | measures vorlon reach at the station |

All three start at 0 (or a documented neutral baseline) and are inert until a source or sink moves them. The B5-0340 recorded station is the locus; the ratings live on that station object in GameState.

## Sources (raise a rating)

- **Station capture**: when a location mini-card is captured by a faction (LocationCard.capturedBy), that faction's rating at that station rises. Human capture raises human influence; shadow capture raises shadow influence; vorlon capture raises vorlon influence. The capture is the source event.
- **Sustained presence**: a faction that holds captured locations near the station across consecutive rounds gains a small incremental rise (presence bleed), capped so a single capture does not dominate.
- **Event cards**: cards that explicitly reference station influence (if any exist in the deck) raise the matching rating. This proposal does not add cards; it only defines the hook so existing or future cards can target it.
- **Tension-matrix interaction**: a faction whose tension is high relative to the station's current defender may gain a small influence edge (pressure), derived from the tension matrix (B5-0376). This is a soft source, not a hard capture.

## Sinks (lower a rating)

- **Station loss**: when a captured location is lost or its capture is removed, the corresponding rating falls.
- **End-of-round decay**: each rating decays slightly at end of round if the owning faction has no captured locations tied to that station (drift toward neutral). Decay is small so a single round of absence does not erase a capture.
- **Counter-influence events**: a faction whose rating is high may be targeted by opponent cards/events that lower it. Again, no cards added here; only the hook.
- **Tension-matrix interaction (reverse)**: a faction whose tension is low relative to the station may lose a small edge (loss of face), derived from the tension matrix.

## End-of-round condition-2 checks

The phrase "condition-2 checks" in the task row is a design placeholder, not a reference to an existing B5-03xx rule. This proposal interprets it as: at end of round, evaluate each rating against a threshold and emit a state change if the threshold is crossed. Concrete checks proposed:

1. **Shadow-War-active threshold**: if shadow influence crosses an upper threshold (proposed: 60/100) AND vorlon influence is below a lower threshold (proposed: 30/100), mark the station as "shadow-active" for the next round. Symmetric rule for vorlon-active. This is the Shadow-War trigger wiring.
2. **Human-defended threshold**: if human influence is above a threshold (proposed: 50/100) and both shadow and vorlon are below their active thresholds, mark the station as "human-secured." This is a read 상태 for the UI readout, not a game-rule change.
3. **Neutral threshold**: if all three ratings are below a low threshold (proposed: 20/100), mark the station as "uncontested." This is the default state and the decay target.

These are proposal-level thresholds. They are not wired to any existing B5-03xx condition-2 rule; they are a suggested convention for the readout tasks and for future card hooks. The exact numbers are placeholders and would be set by the GSS-measurable design-intent class when a card-level hook is added.

## Shadow-War trigger wiring

Shadow War, when active, is a faction-vs-faction struggle over station influence. This proposal wires it as a read-only state derived from the three ratings, not as a new game action:

- **Shadow War active** (proposal): shadow influence > 60 AND vorlon influence < 30 → station is shadow-active.
- **Vorlon War active** (proposal): vorlon influence > 60 AND shadow influence < 30 → station is vorlon-active.
- **Cold War** (proposal): both shadow and vorlon above 40 but neither above 60 → station is contested-but-stable.
- **Human-secured** (proposal): human > 50 AND both shadow and vorlon < active thresholds → human holds the station.

The readout tasks (B5-0427/B5-0429) would display the active state derived from these thresholds. No card or action is added here; the trigger is a read of the ratings at end of round.

## Tension-matrix interaction

The tension matrix (B5-0376) already exists in GameState. This proposal ties it to station influence as a soft modifier:

- A faction with high tension toward the station's current dominant faction gains a small influence edge (pressure bonus) on source events.
- A faction with low tension toward the station's current dominant faction loses a small edge (loss-of-face penalty) on source events.
- The tension matrix is read, not written, by this proposal. No tension-matrix edits are proposed.

This keeps the tension matrix as the existing B5-0376 artifact and adds a read path from station influence back to tension-derived soft modifiers. The readout tasks would display both the raw tension numbers (B5-0429) and the derived station influence state (B5-0427/B5-0429).

## B5-0354 trap (no Support Babylon 5 rewiring)

B5-0354 is the "Support Babylon 5" card effect that gives the human player-side a specific benefit when Babylon 5 is supported. This proposal MUST NOT rewire that effect. Specifically:

- Support Babylon 5 (B5-0354) remains a player-side card effect with its existing behavior. This proposal does not touch it.
- Station influence ratings are separate from the Support Babylon 5 trigger. A high human influence rating does NOT auto-trigger Support Babylon 5, and Support Babylon 5 does NOT auto-raise human influence. They are parallel read paths, not a rewrite.
- If a future card wants to connect station influence to Support Babylon 5, that is a card-design decision outside this proposal.

This trap is recorded so the next worker (B5-0427/B5-0429 readout, or a future card-hook task) does not accidentally fold station influence into the Support Babylon 5 player-side effects.

## Recommendations

1. **Adopt the three-rating model** (human/shadow/vorlon, 0-100, inert from day one) as the station-influence design target. It gives the readout tasks (B5-0427/B5-0429) a concrete thing to read and display, and it gives future card hooks a defined namespace.
2. **Wire the readout tasks first** (B5-0427 captured-location + war-state, then B5-0429 station + tension) against the inert ratings — they would display "0 / uncontested" until a source moves the ratings. This validates the read path without changing game behavior.
3. **Add card hooks later** — once the readout tasks confirm the read path, add one or two station-influence cards (sources/sinks) that move the ratings, and wire the end-of-round condition-2 checks (thresholds) to those card-driven changes. The GSS-measurable design-intent class sets the final thresholds at that point.
4. **Keep B5-0354 isolated** — document the no-rewire trap in the engine/model README so the next card-design worker does not fold station influence into Support Babylon 5.
